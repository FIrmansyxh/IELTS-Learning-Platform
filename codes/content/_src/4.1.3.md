## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Memahami dan memakai **kosakata General Training**: kehidupan sehari-hari, tempat tinggal, pekerjaan, layanan, belanja, perjalanan, dan komunitas.
2. Memakai **kolokasi dan frasa tetap** yang muncul di **Reading Section 1–2** (pengumuman, brosur, iklan lowongan) dan **Writing Task 1** (surat).
3. Membedakan **register formal, semi-formal, dan informal** pada kata dan frasa yang sama.
4. Menguasai **singkatan, istilah layanan, dan phrasal verbs** yang sering muncul.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - General Training menguji bahasa **dunia nyata**: surat keluhan, lamaran kerja, brosur, jadwal, pengumuman.
> - Pelajari kata **berdasarkan situasi** dan **frasa tetap** (*apply for a position*, *submit a complaint*, *a refund is available*).
> - Perhatikan **register**: *contact* (formal) vs. *get in touch* (semi-formal) vs. *drop me a line* (informal).

---

## 1. Mengapa Kosakata Situasi Nyata?

Pada General Training, Reading Section 1 berisi **teks sehari-hari** dan Writing Task 1 adalah **surat**. Kosakata yang dipakai adalah **kosakata layanan dan komunitas**, bukan istilah ilmiah.

| Bagian tes | Contoh teks/tugas | Kosakata yang dibutuhkan |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading Section 1** | Iklan, pengumuman, jadwal | *vacancy, deposit, valid, per person* |
| **Reading Section 2** | Deskripsi pekerjaan, kebijakan | *probation, shift, qualifications* |
| **Writing Task 1** | Surat keluhan/permintaan/lamaran | *refund, replacement, apply, enquire* |
| **Speaking Part 1** | Rumah, pekerjaan, kota | *rent, commute, neighbourhood* |
| **Listening Section 1–2** | Pemesanan, informasi fasilitas | *booking, fee, membership* |

## 3. Register: Formal, Semi-Formal, Informal

:::compare
Formal | contact; request; I would be grateful if you could
Semi-formal | get in touch with; ask for; Could you possibly
Informal | drop me a line; ask; Could you
:::

| Makna | Formal | Semi-formal | Informal |
|---|---|---|---|
| menghubungi | contact | get in touch with | drop me a line |
| meminta | request | ask for | ask |
| memberi tahu | inform | let (someone) know | tell |
| membantu | assist | help | give a hand |
| memulai | commence | begin / start | kick off |
| mengeluh | lodge a complaint | complain | moan |
| mohon dibalas | I look forward to your reply. | Hope to hear from you. | Write back soon! |
| terima kasih | I would be grateful. | Thank you very much. | Thanks a lot! |
| mengundang | I would like to invite you. | I'd like to invite you. | Fancy coming? |

:::tips-examiner
Dalam **Writing Task 1 General Training**, register adalah bagian dari **Task Achievement**. Satu surat harus konsisten dari salam sampai penutup. Menggunakan *I wish to complain* di awal lalu *Thanks a lot!* di akhir adalah ketidakkonsistenan yang menurunkan skor. Lihat sub-modul 3.3.3 untuk struktur lengkap surat.
:::

## 4. Frasa Tetap untuk Surat dan Pengumuman

### 4.1 Frasa untuk surat

| Tujuan | Frasa |
|---|---|
| **Membuka** | I am writing to enquire about..., I am writing to complain about..., I am writing to apply for... |
| **Menjelaskan** | Unfortunately..., As a result..., This has caused me... |
| **Meminta** | I would be grateful if you could..., Could you please..., I would appreciate it if... |
| **Menawarkan** | I would be happy to..., Please let me know if... |
| **Menutup** | I look forward to hearing from you., Thank you for your attention to this matter. |

## 7. Variasi Contoh (dari mudah ke sulit)

**Level 1: kata tunggal**

1. The room is **vacant**.
2. Please keep the **receipt**.
3. The deadline is on **Friday**.

**Level 2: kolokasi**

4. I would like to **apply for** the position of receptionist.
5. The **deposit** is **non-refundable**.
6. You can **sign up for** the course at the front desk.

**Level 3: konteks lengkap**

7. The item arrived **faulty**, so I would be grateful if you could send a **replacement** or issue a full **refund**.
8. The **tenant** must give one month's **notice** before moving out, and the **deposit** will be returned after an inspection.
9. The course is **free of charge** for residents, but places are limited and offered on a **first-come, first-served basis**.

## 8. Contoh Jawaban Model: Penerapan dalam Surat

**Situasi:** *Kamu membeli laptop yang rusak. Tulis kepada toko.*

- **Band 5:** I want my money back. The laptop is broken. You must send me new one quick.
- **Band 6:** I am writing to complain about a laptop I bought last week. It is faulty, so I would like a refund or a replacement as soon as possible.
- **Band 7:** I am writing to lodge a complaint about a laptop that I purchased from your website last week. Unfortunately, the screen was cracked on arrival, which has caused me considerable inconvenience. I would be grateful if you could send a replacement or issue a full refund at your earliest convenience.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) **Frasa tetap** (*lodge a complaint, issue a full refund, at your earliest convenience*), (2) **register formal konsisten**, (3) **kolokasi akurat** (*faulty, cracked on arrival, considerable inconvenience*), (4) kalimat gabungan yang rapi.

## 9. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

:::kesalahan-umum
Kesalahan paling sering pada kosakata situasi nyata adalah **preposisi dan struktur yang diterjemahkan langsung dari bahasa Indonesia**: *apply to* (benar: *apply for*), *discuss about* (benar: *discuss*), *return back* (benar: *return*), *explain me* (benar: *explain to me*). Hafalkan kata **bersama preposisinya**, dan jaga **register** surat dari salam sampai penutup.
:::

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **I want** a refund (surat formal) | **I would like to request** a refund | Register |
| 2 | I **look forward to hear** | I look forward to **hearing** | Gerund setelah *to* |
| 3 | **Informations** about the course | **Information** about | Uncountable |
| 4 | **Apply to** a job | **Apply for** a job | Preposisi |
| 5 | **Explain me** the problem | **Explain** the problem **to me** | Struktur |
| 6 | **Discuss about** the issue | **Discuss** the issue | Tanpa *about* |
| 7 | **Return back** the item | **Return** the item | Redundan |
| 8 | **Enquiry** (kata kerja) | **enquire** | Kelas kata |
| 9 | A **discount of 20** | A discount of 20**%** | Satuan |
| 10 | **Reserve** vs **booking** tertukar | *reserve* (verb), *booking* (noun) | Kelas kata |
| 11 | **Cancel off** | **Cancel** | Redundan |
| 12 | **Slang** di surat formal | Gunakan kata formal | Register |

## 10. Strategi IELTS: Cara Belajar dan Memakai

### 10.1 Metode belajar

| Metode | Cara |
|---|---|
| **Kelompokkan per situasi** | Pelajari 8 kata per situasi (sewa, kerja, keluhan) |
| **Gunakan dalam surat contoh** | Tulis satu surat per situasi |
| **Baca brosur dan iklan asli** | Catat frasa tetap (*valid until, subject to availability*) |
| **Catat register** | Tandai formal, semi-formal, informal |
| **Pengulangan berjarak** | 1, 3, 7, 14 hari |

### 10.2 Saat tes

- **Reading:** kenali frasa tetap dan singkatan agar tidak tersandung di teks sehari-hari.
- **Writing:** pilih register di awal dan pertahankan; pakai 3–5 frasa tetap yang kamu kuasai.
- **Speaking Part 1:** pakai kata situasi nyata (*rent, commute, neighbourhood*) dengan kolokasi benar.
- **Listening:** perhatikan angka, nama, dan istilah layanan.

## 11. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**

- Kosakata General Training berpusat pada **situasi nyata** (sewa, kerja, layanan, perjalanan).
- Pelajari **frasa tetap**, **singkatan**, dan **phrasal verbs**.
- Jaga **register** konsisten (formal, semi-formal, informal).
- Hindari *apply to a job*, *discuss about*, *return back*.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *Apa arti "vacancy"?*
2. *Mana yang benar: "apply to a job" atau "apply for a job"?*
3. *Ubah ke formal: "I want a refund."*
4. *Apa arti "non-refundable"?*
5. *Apa kepanjangan RSVP?*

**Kunci:** 1. Lowongan. 2. apply for a job. 3. I would like to request a refund. 4. Tidak dapat dikembalikan. 5. Répondez s'il vous plaît (mohon konfirmasi kehadiran).
