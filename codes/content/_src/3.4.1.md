## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menjelaskan format **Speaking Part 1** dan apa yang dinilai penguji.
2. Menjawab dengan rumus **DRE: Direct answer + Reason + Example** secara natural, dalam 2–4 kalimat.
3. Memilih **tense** yang tepat sesuai jenis pertanyaan dan memperpanjang jawaban tanpa menghafal.
4. Menjaga **fluency** (tidak berhenti lama), memperbaiki diri secara wajar, dan mengatasi saat kehabisan kata.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Part 1 = **wawancara 4–5 menit** tentang topik akrab (diri, rumah, pekerjaan/studi, hobi).
> - Jawab **langsung** dulu, lalu beri **alasan**, lalu **contoh singkat**: *Direct answer + Reason + Example.*
> - Jawaban ideal **2–4 kalimat** (sekitar 15–30 detik). Satu kata ("Yes.") terlalu pendek; monolog panjang berlebihan.

## 1. Gambaran Tes Speaking

| Bagian | Durasi | Isi |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** | 4–5 menit | Pertanyaan singkat tentang topik akrab |
| **Part 2** | 3–4 menit | Cue card: bicara 1–2 menit tentang satu topik |
| **Part 3** | 4–5 menit | Diskusi abstrak yang berhubungan dengan Part 2 |

Total sekitar **11–14 menit**, direkam, dengan satu penguji.

### 1.1 Empat kriteria (bobot sama, masing-masing 25%)

| Kriteria | Yang dinilai | Yang menurunkan skor |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluency and Coherence** | Lancar, ide teratur, penghubung wajar | Jeda panjang, banyak pengulangan, jawaban terputus |
| **Lexical Resource** | Kosakata tepat dan bervariasi, parafrase | Kata yang sama berulang, kata salah |
| **Grammatical Range and Accuracy** | Variasi struktur, akurasi | Hanya kalimat sangat pendek, banyak kesalahan dasar |
| **Pronunciation** | Mudah dipahami, tekanan kata, intonasi | Bunyi sulit dipahami, intonasi datar |

## 2. Format Part 1

| Hal | Keterangan |
|---|---|
| Jumlah | Sekitar **10–12 pertanyaan** dalam **2–3 topik** |
| Pembuka | Penguji menanyakan nama, asal; lalu topik pertama biasanya **pekerjaan/studi, rumah/kota asal** |
| Topik berikutnya | Hobi, makanan, teknologi, cuaca, transportasi, olahraga, buku, musik, kesehatan, dan sebagainya |
| Gaya | Percakapan; tidak ada persiapan |
| Tidak ada **jawaban benar atau salah** | Yang dinilai adalah **bahasa**, bukan isi |

**Contoh pertanyaan umum:**

| Topik | Contoh pertanyaan |
|---|---|
| Diri/pekerjaan | *Do you work or are you a student? Why did you choose that?* |
| Rumah | *Do you live in a house or an apartment? What do you like most about it?* |
| Kota asal | *Where are you from? Is it a good place for young people?* |
| Hobi | *What do you do in your free time? Has it changed since you were a child?* |
| Makanan | *What kind of food do you like to eat? Do you cook?* |
| Teknologi | *How often do you use your phone? What do you use it for?* |

## 3. Rumus DRE: Direct Answer + Reason + Example

:::flow
Direct answer | Jawab langsung
Reason | Alasan dengan because
Example | Contoh pendek atau detail
:::

| Langkah | Fungsi | Contoh (pertanyaan: *Do you like cooking?*) |
|---|---|---|
| **D**irect answer | Menjawab langsung | *Yes, I really enjoy it.* |
| **R**eason | Alasan atau penjelasan | *It helps me relax after a long day, and I like trying new recipes.* |
| **E**xample | Contoh pendek atau detail | *Last weekend, for instance, I tried making a Thai curry for my family.* |

**Jawaban lengkap:** *Yes, I really enjoy it. It helps me relax after a long day, and I like trying new recipes. Last weekend, for instance, I tried making a Thai curry for my family.*

### 3.1 Variasi alat pengembang jawaban

| Alat | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Alasan** | *because / since / as* | I prefer walking **because** it clears my mind. |
| **Contoh** | *for example / for instance / such as* | I enjoy sports, **such as** badminton and swimming. |
| **Kontras** | *but / although / however / whereas* | I like cities, **but** I find them noisy. |
| **Perbandingan masa lalu** | *used to / when I was...* | I **used to** play outside every afternoon. |
| **Perasaan** | *I feel..., it makes me...* | It **makes me** feel relaxed. |
| **Kualifikasi** | *usually / sometimes / it depends* | I **usually** study in the library. |

:::tips-examiner
Jangan menjawab dengan **satu kata** ("Yes", "No", "Football") dan jangan menghafal jawaban panjang. Penguji bisa mengenali jawaban hafalan dari intonasi dan ketidaksesuaian dengan pertanyaan. Rumus DRE memberi kerangka, tetapi isinya harus **spontan dan sesuai pertanyaan**. Target: **2–4 kalimat** per jawaban.
:::

## 4. Memilih Tense yang Tepat

Tense di jawaban harus **mengikuti pertanyaan**. Ini juga menunjukkan akurasi grammar.

| Jenis pertanyaan | Tense | Contoh pertanyaan → jawaban |
|---|---|---|
| **Kebiasaan / fakta sekarang** | Present Simple | *What do you do in your free time?* → I **usually watch** films. |
| **Situasi sekarang** | Present Continuous | *Are you studying anything now?* → I'**m taking** an English course. |
| **Masa lalu** | Past Simple | *Did you enjoy school?* → Yes, I **did**. I **loved** the science lessons. |
| **Pengalaman** | Present Perfect | *Have you ever traveled abroad?* → Yes, I'**ve been** to Singapore twice. |
| **Kebiasaan masa lalu** | used to / would | *What games did you play?* → I **used to** play marbles. |
| **Masa depan** | will / going to | *Will you work in your hometown?* → I think I'**ll** move to a big city. |
| **Perbandingan** | Comparatives | *Is your city bigger than before?* → It's **much busier** than ten years ago. |

Lihat Modul 1.1 untuk rumus tiap tense.

## 5. Mengembangkan Jawaban Tanpa Menghafal

### 5.1 Pertanyaan "Yes/No"

| Pertanyaan | Jawaban pendek (lemah) | Jawaban berkembang (kuat) |
|---|---|---|
| *Do you like reading?* | Yes. | Yes, I do. I try to read for about half an hour before bed, mostly novels, because it helps me switch off. |
| *Do you work or study?* | I study. | I'm a student at the moment. I'm studying nursing, which I chose because I've always wanted a job that helps people. |

### 5.2 Pertanyaan "Why"

*Pola:* **Main reason + because + detail + contoh.** *I chose nursing mainly because I want a stable career. My aunt is a nurse, and seeing her work inspired me.*

### 5.3 Pertanyaan "Do you prefer A or B?"

*Pola:* **Pilih satu + alasan + (pengakuan sisi lain).** *I prefer cooking at home because it's cheaper and healthier, although I do enjoy eating out occasionally.*

### 5.4 Pertanyaan masa lalu ("Did you...? When you were a child...")

*Pola:* **Past Simple + detail + perasaan.** *I used to play badminton with my cousins every afternoon. It was the highlight of my day.*

## 6. Menjaga Fluency

### 6.1 Penghubung dan penanda wacana yang natural

| Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Memulai** | Well, ... / Actually, ... / To be honest, ... / Let me think... |
| **Menambah** | Also, ... / Besides, ... / What's more, ... |
| **Kontras** | But, ... / On the other hand, ... / However, ... |
| **Alasan** | The reason is that ... / That's because ... |
| **Memberi contoh** | For example, ... / Like, ... |
| **Mengakhiri** | So, ... / Overall, ... / That's why ... |

### 6.2 Mengatasi saat kehabisan kata

| Situasi | Apa yang dilakukan |
|---|---|
| **Lupa kata** | Parafrase: *a thing you use to cut paper* (scissors) |
| **Butuh waktu berpikir** | *That's an interesting question. Let me think...* |
| **Salah ucap** | Perbaiki wajar: *I go... I went there last week.* |
| **Tidak paham pertanyaan** | *Sorry, could you repeat that, please?* (satu-dua kali masih wajar) |
| **Tidak punya pendapat** | Tetap jawab: *I haven't thought about it much, but I guess...* |

:::kesalahan-umum
**Jeda panjang dan kata isian berlebihan** (*errr, ummm* terus-menerus) menurunkan Fluency. Ganti dengan frasa pengisi yang sopan (*Well, ... / Let me see...*). Namun menunggu sejenak untuk berpikir itu wajar; yang bermasalah adalah **diam yang terlalu lama** atau berhenti mencari kata yang sempurna. Lebih baik memakai kata sederhana dengan lancar.
:::

## 7. Bank Kosakata Topik Part 1

| Topik | Ungkapan berguna |
|---|---|
| **Studi/pekerjaan** | I'm majoring in..., It's quite demanding, a steady income, I enjoy working with people |
| **Rumah** | a cozy place, spacious, in a quiet neighbourhood, close to public transport |
| **Kota asal** | a bustling city, a small town, famous for..., lots of things to do |
| **Hobi** | I'm really into..., it helps me unwind, a great way to relieve stress |
| **Makanan** | spicy, home-cooked, I have a sweet tooth, a balanced diet |
| **Teknologi** | I rely on my phone, convenient, it saves time, I'm hooked on... |
| **Cuaca** | It's hot and humid, the rainy season, I prefer cooler weather |
| **Transportasi** | I commute by motorbike, heavy traffic, public transport is reliable |

**Catatan:** kosakata sederhana yang **tepat** lebih baik daripada kata sulit yang salah. Idiom boleh dipakai **sesekali** dan hanya bila kamu yakin artinya.

## 8. Pronunciation untuk Part 1

| Aspek | Penjelasan | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Word stress** | Suku kata yang ditekan | *PHOtograph*, *phoTOgrapher*, *photoGRAphic* |
| **Intonasi** | Nada naik-turun menunjukkan makna dan perasaan | Naik pada pertanyaan yes/no; turun pada pernyataan |
| **Bunyi akhir** | Konsonan akhir sering hilang | Ucapkan akhiran **-s, -ed, -t, -d**: *worked*, *cats* |
| **Bunyi sulit** | /θ/ (think), /ð/ (this), /v/ vs /f/, /ʃ/ (she) | Latih pasangan minimal: *thin/tin, very/ferry* |
| **Linking** | Menghubungkan kata | *an apple* → "a-napple" |

**Latihan:** rekam jawaban 1 menit di ponsel, dengarkan ulang, dan catat bunyi akhir yang hilang atau kata yang tidak jelas.

## 9. Contoh Jawaban Model: Tiga Level Band

**Pertanyaan:** *What do you usually do in your free time?*

- **Band 5:** I play game. I watch TV. I like. It is fun. Sometimes I go outside.
- **Band 6:** In my free time, I usually watch films or play badminton with my friends. I like them because they are relaxing. I also sometimes go to the mall.
- **Band 7:** Well, it depends on the day, but I'm really into badminton, so I usually play with a few friends at a local court on Saturday mornings. It's a great way to unwind after a busy week, and it keeps me fit. If the weather is bad, I tend to stay in and watch films instead.

**Pertanyaan:** *Do you like cooking?*

- **Band 5:** Yes. I cook sometimes. It is good for me and my family.
- **Band 6:** Yes, I like cooking. I cook dinner for my family at weekends, and I enjoy trying new recipes from the internet.
- **Band 7:** Absolutely. I find it really relaxing, especially after a stressful day. I'm not an expert, but I've recently been trying out recipes from different countries, and last weekend I made a Thai curry for my family, which turned out better than I'd expected.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) Jawaban **dikembangkan** dengan alasan dan contoh (DRE), (2) tense **sesuai** (present simple, present perfect continuous, past simple), (3) **penghubung natural** (*Well, especially, If..., instead*), (4) kosakata **tepat** (*unwind, keeps me fit, turned out*).

## 10. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Menjawab satu kata ("Yes.") | Tambah alasan dan contoh | Terlalu pendek |
| 2 | Menghafal jawaban panjang | Jawab spontan dengan kerangka DRE | Tidak sesuai pertanyaan |
| 3 | **I am like football** | I **like** football | *Like* sudah kata kerja |
| 4 | **I very like** | I **really like** / I like it **very much** | Urutan kata |
| 5 | **Yesterday I go** | Yesterday I **went** | Tense lampau |
| 6 | **My hobby is play badminton** | My hobby **is playing** badminton / I **play** badminton | Bentuk -ing setelah *is* |
| 7 | **I have 20 years old** | I **am** 20 years old | Umur dengan *be* |
| 8 | Diam lama sebelum menjawab | *Well, let me think...* | Menjaga fluency |
| 9 | Meminta pengulangan terus-menerus | Pahami dari konteks; maksimal satu-dua kali | Menunjukkan kesulitan |
| 10 | Menjawab pertanyaan yang tidak ditanya | Fokus pada pertanyaan | Off-topic |
| 11 | **Because ..., so ...** dalam satu kalimat | Pilih salah satu | Penghubung ganda |
| 12 | Intonasi datar | Variasikan nada | Tidak ekspresif |

## 11. Strategi IELTS: Persiapan dan Hari Tes

### 11.1 Latihan sehari-hari (15 menit)

| Latihan | Cara | Tujuan |
|---|---|---|
| **Jawab 5 pertanyaan** dengan DRE, direkam | Pilih satu topik tiap hari | Fluency dan struktur |
| **Dengarkan rekaman**, catat kesalahan | Fokus pada tense dan bunyi akhir | Akurasi |
| **Parafrase** kata yang sering diulang | Buat daftar sinonim | Lexical Resource |
| **Shadowing** (menirukan) pembicara native | Pilih video pendek | Pronunciation dan intonasi |

### 11.2 Saat tes

- **Mulai dengan senyum dan kontak mata**; kepercayaan diri membantu penilaian.
- **Dengarkan pertanyaan** dengan teliti; perhatikan **kata kerja dan tense**.
- **Jawab 2–4 kalimat**, lalu berhenti; penguji akan melanjutkan.
- **Jujur**: jawab sesuai keadaanmu; yang dinilai adalah bahasa.
- **Jangan khawatir** soal pertanyaan yang aneh; tetap jawab dengan kerangka DRE.

## 12. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**

- Part 1: 4–5 menit, topik akrab, 2–4 kalimat per jawaban.
- Rumus **DRE**: Direct answer + Reason + Example.
- Tense mengikuti pertanyaan; penghubung natural; parafrase saat lupa kata.
- Empat kriteria: Fluency/Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammar, Pronunciation.
- Jangan menghafal; jangan menjawab satu kata.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *Apa kepanjangan DRE?*
2. *Berapa panjang jawaban Part 1 yang ideal?*
3. *Tense apa yang cocok untuk "What did you do last weekend?"*
4. *Apa yang bisa kamu lakukan bila lupa sebuah kata?*
5. *Mengapa menghafal jawaban berisiko?*

**Kunci:** 1. Direct answer, Reason, Example. 2. 2–4 kalimat (sekitar 15–30 detik). 3. Past Simple. 4. Parafrase atau jelaskan dengan kata lain. 5. Penguji mengenali jawaban hafalan dan jawabannya mungkin tidak sesuai pertanyaan.
