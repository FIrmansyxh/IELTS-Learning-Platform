## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Membedakan **countable** dan **uncountable noun**, lalu membentuk **plural** yang benar (beraturan dan tak beraturan).
2. Memilih **pronoun** yang tepat (subject, object, possessive, reflexive) dan menghindari pengulangan kata benda.
3. Memakai **a / an / the / tanpa artikel** dengan benar.
4. Memakai **quantifier** (many, much, a few, a little, some, any) sesuai jenis kata benda.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Kata benda **countable** bisa dihitung dan punya bentuk jamak (*book → books*); **uncountable** tidak (*information, advice*).
> - **a/an** = satu hal yang belum spesifik; **the** = hal yang sudah jelas bagi pembicara dan pendengar.
> - Bahasa Indonesia tidak mewajibkan bentuk jamak dan artikel. Bahasa Inggris **mewajibkan** keduanya, dan inilah sumber kesalahan paling sering di Writing.

## 1. Noun (Kata Benda)

### 1.1 Jenis kata benda

| Jenis | Penjelasan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Countable** | Dapat dihitung, punya tunggal dan jamak | a book, two books, an idea, three ideas |
| **Uncountable** | Tidak dapat dihitung satu per satu, tidak ada bentuk jamak | water, rice, information, advice, furniture |
| **Proper noun** | Nama khusus, huruf awal kapital | Indonesia, Jakarta, Rina, Monday |
| **Abstract noun** | Konsep, tidak berwujud | happiness, freedom, education |
| **Collective noun** | Kelompok | team, family, government, audience |

### 1.2 Kata benda uncountable yang sering salah

Banyak kata yang di bahasa Indonesia terasa bisa dihitung ternyata **uncountable** dalam bahasa Inggris:

| Uncountable | Cara menghitungnya | Salah → Benar |
|---|---|---|
| information | a piece of information | *informations* (salah) → **information** |
| advice | a piece of advice | *advices* (salah) → **advice** |
| furniture | a piece of furniture | *furnitures* (salah) → **furniture** |
| equipment | an item of equipment | *equipments* (salah) → **equipment** |
| homework | an assignment | *homeworks* (salah) → **homework** |
| news | a piece of news | *a news* (salah) → **a piece of news** |
| luggage / baggage | a piece of luggage | *luggages* (salah) → **luggage** |
| traffic, weather, progress, research, knowledge, music, money, work | — | semuanya tidak berbentuk jamak |

:::kesalahan-umum
*I need some **informations** about the course* dan *She gave me many **advices*** adalah kesalahan paling sering di Writing IELTS. Ingat: **information, advice, equipment, homework, furniture, news** tidak pernah memakai **-s** dan tidak memakai **a/an** langsung.
:::

### 1.3 Aturan bentuk jamak beraturan

| Aturan | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Kebanyakan + **-s** | book → book**s**, idea → idea**s** |
| Berakhiran **-s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh** + **-es** | bus → bus**es**, box → box**es**, watch → watch**es**, dish → dish**es** |
| Konsonan + **y** → **-ies** | city → cit**ies**, country → countr**ies** |
| Vokal + **y** + **-s** | day → day**s**, key → key**s** |
| Banyak kata berakhiran **-f/-fe** → **-ves** | knife → kni**ves**, leaf → lea**ves**, life → li**ves** |
| Kata berakhiran **-o** (konsonan + o) umumnya + **-es** | potato → potato**es**, tomato → tomato**es** (tetapi *photo → photos*, *piano → pianos*) |

### 1.4 Bentuk jamak tak beraturan

| Tunggal | Jamak | Catatan |
|---|---|---|
| man | men | |
| woman | women | |
| child | children | |
| person | people | *persons* sangat formal |
| foot | feet | |
| tooth | teeth | |
| mouse | mice | |
| sheep | sheep | sama |
| fish | fish | sama (*fishes* untuk jenis) |
| analysis | analyses | dari Latin/Yunani |
| crisis | crises | |
| phenomenon | phenomena | |
| criterion | criteria | |

### 1.5 Variasi contoh

**Level 1: dasar**
1. I have **two brothers** and **one sister**.
2. The **children** are playing in the **garden**.
3. She bought some **tomatoes** and **potatoes**.

**Level 2: contoh uncountable dan hitungan**
4. Could you give me some **advice** about studying abroad?
5. The **news** was surprising, and the **people** in the room were silent.
6. We need a lot of **equipment** for the experiment, but only a few **items** are available.

**Level 3: konteks akademik**
7. The **research** shows that many **students** struggle with time management.
8. Several **phenomena** cannot be explained by current **theories**.
9. Heavy **traffic** is one of the main **causes** of air **pollution** in big **cities**.

## 2. Pronoun (Kata Ganti)

### 2.1 Tabel pronoun lengkap

| Subjek | Objek | Possessive adjective | Possessive pronoun | Reflexive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| you (jamak) | you | your | yours | yourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

### 2.2 Fungsi tiap kolom

| Jenis | Posisi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject** | Sebelum kata kerja | **She** works in a bank. |
| **Object** | Setelah kata kerja atau preposisi | The manager called **her**. Give it to **them**. |
| **Possessive adjective** | Sebelum kata benda | **My** brother is a doctor. |
| **Possessive pronoun** | Menggantikan "possessive + noun" | This bag is **mine**. (= my bag) |
| **Reflexive** | Subjek dan objek sama | He taught **himself** to code. |

:::tips-examiner
Untuk menaikkan **Lexical Resource** dan **Cohesion** di Writing, hindari mengulang kata benda yang sama. Ganti dengan pronoun: *The government introduced a new policy. **It** aims to reduce pollution, and **its** effects are already visible.* Pastikan setiap pronoun jelas merujuk pada satu kata benda (tidak ambigu).
:::

### 2.3 *It's* vs. *its*, *their* vs. *they're* vs. *there*

| Kata | Arti | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| it's | it is / it has | **It's** late. **It's** been a long day. |
| its | milik "it" | The company changed **its** logo. |
| their | milik "they" | **Their** house is near the station. |
| they're | they are | **They're** waiting outside. |
| there | di sana / ada | **There** is a café **there**. |
| your / you're | milik kamu / kamu adalah | **Your** essay is good. **You're** improving. |

### 2.4 Variasi contoh

**Level 1:** *She* likes *him*, but *he* doesn't know *it*.
**Level 2:** My laptop is old, so I'm going to buy a new **one**. Yours is newer than **mine**.
**Level 3:** Students should take responsibility for **their** learning. If **they** do not motivate **themselves**, no teacher can do it for **them**.

## 3. Articles (a / an / the / tanpa artikel)

### 3.1 Aturan dasar

| Artikel | Dipakai untuk | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | Satu hal pertama kali disebut; bunyi awal konsonan | **a** book, **a** university (/juː/), **a** European |
| **an** | Satu hal pertama kali disebut; bunyi awal **vokal** | **an** apple, **an** hour (h tidak dibaca), **an** MBA |
| **the** | Hal tertentu yang sudah jelas bagi pembicara dan pendengar | **The** book on the table is mine. |
| **tanpa artikel (Ø)** | Jamak umum, uncountable umum, banyak nama | **Ø** Students need **Ø** advice. |

> **Penting:** aturan *a/an* berdasarkan **bunyi**, bukan huruf. *an hour* (bunyi /aʊ/), *a university* (bunyi /j/).

### 3.2 Kapan memakai *the*?

| Situasi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Sudah disebut sebelumnya | I bought **a** laptop and **a** phone. **The** laptop was expensive. |
| Hanya ada satu di konteksnya | **The** sun rises in the east. Open **the** window, please. |
| Dengan superlative dan *first/last/only* | She is **the** best student. He was **the** first to arrive. |
| Sungai, samudra, pegunungan, kelompok pulau | **the** Nile, **the** Pacific, **the** Alps, **the** Maldives |
| Negara berbentuk kumpulan/republik | **the** United States, **the** Netherlands, **the** Philippines |
| Institusi dan jabatan unik | **the** government, **the** president, **the** Internet |
| Generalisasi dengan kata sifat | **the** rich, **the** elderly, **the** unemployed |

### 3.3 Kapan **tidak** memakai artikel?

| Situasi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Jamak atau uncountable bersifat umum | **Ø** Dogs are loyal. **Ø** Water is essential. |
| Nama orang, kota, negara (tunggal) | **Ø** Rina lives in **Ø** Indonesia. |
| Bahasa, mata pelajaran, olahraga | I study **Ø** English. She plays **Ø** badminton. |
| Waktu makan, kebanyakan transportasi dengan *by* | We have **Ø** lunch at noon. I go **by** bus. |
| Institusi dengan fungsi utama (pergi untuk tujuan fungsinya) | go to **Ø** school / **Ø** university / **Ø** hospital / **Ø** church |

**Perbandingan:** *He goes to **Ø** school every day.* (sebagai murid) vs. *His father went to **the** school to meet the teacher.* (ke bangunan sekolahnya)

### 3.4 Tabel keputusan cepat

| Pertanyaan | Jawaban | Artikel |
|---|---|---|
| Apakah tunggal countable? | Ya, pertama kali / belum spesifik | **a/an** |
| Apakah tunggal countable? | Ya, sudah spesifik | **the** |
| Apakah jamak/uncountable? | Umum | **Ø** |
| Apakah jamak/uncountable? | Spesifik | **the** |

### 3.5 Variasi contoh

**Level 1:** I have **a** cat. **The** cat is black. She wants **an** orange. We saw **the** moon.
**Level 2:** **Ø** Children need **Ø** love and **Ø** attention. **The** children in my class are very active.
**Level 3:** **The** number of **Ø** students studying abroad has increased. **The** main reason is **the** availability of **Ø** scholarships, which give **Ø** students from **Ø** developing countries **an** opportunity to get **a** world-class education.

## 4. Quantifier (Penentu Jumlah)

| Quantifier | Dipakai dengan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **many** | countable jamak (biasanya negatif/tanya) | I don't have **many** friends here. |
| **much** | uncountable (biasanya negatif/tanya) | We don't have **much** time. |
| **a lot of / lots of** | countable dan uncountable | **A lot of** people / **A lot of** water |
| **a few** | countable jamak (cukup) | I have **a few** ideas. |
| **few** | countable jamak (hampir tidak ada, negatif) | **Few** people know the answer. |
| **a little** | uncountable (cukup) | I have **a little** money left. |
| **little** | uncountable (hampir tidak ada, negatif) | There is **little** hope. |
| **some** | kalimat positif | I bought **some** apples. |
| **any** | negatif dan tanya | I don't have **any** questions. Do you have **any** milk? |
| **fewer / less** | fewer + countable; less + uncountable | **fewer** cars, **less** pollution |

## 5. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I have three **book**. | I have three **books**. | Jumlah > 1 wajib jamak. Bahasa Indonesia memakai reduplikasi atau tidak sama sekali. |
| 2 | She gave me some **advices**. | some **advice** | *Advice* uncountable. |
| 3 | I need **a information**. | I need **some information**. / **a piece of** information | Uncountable tidak memakai *a/an*. |
| 4 | He is **a** honest man. | He is **an** honest man. | Berdasarkan bunyi: *h* tidak dibaca. |
| 5 | I go to **the** school every day. | I go to school every day. | Fungsi utama institusi tanpa artikel. |
| 6 | **The** life is difficult. | **Ø** Life is difficult. | Generalisasi tanpa *the*. |
| 7 | I bought a book. **A** book was interesting. | **The** book was interesting. | Sudah disebut = *the*. |
| 8 | There are **much** people here. | **many** people / **a lot of** people | *People* countable jamak. |
| 9 | There is **a few** water. | **a little** water | Water uncountable. |
| 10 | The **peoples** in my country are friendly. | The **people** in my country are friendly. | *People* sudah jamak. |
| 11 | He hurt **him**. (dirinya sendiri) | He hurt **himself**. | Subjek = objek, pakai reflexive. |
| 12 | This is **my** book, not **your**. | not **yours**. | Menggantikan "your book" memakai possessive pronoun. |

## 6. Strategi IELTS

- **Writing (semua task):** cek bagian akhir setiap kalimat. Apakah semua kata benda countable tunggal punya **a/an/the/possessive**? Apakah semua kata benda jamak berakhiran **-s**? Kesalahan ini muncul puluhan kali dan menurunkan **Grammatical Accuracy**.
- **Writing Task 2:** kalimat generalisasi memakai **Ø + jamak** (*Governments should invest in schools.*) bukan *The governments*.
- **Listening (Form Completion):** perhatikan *a/an* sebelum celah. *an ___* berarti kata jawabannya berawal bunyi vokal. Plural pada celah berarti jawabannya jamak (*two ___ → tickets*).
- **Reading (Summary Completion):** artikel dan jumlah di sekitar celah membantu menebak jenis kata benda (jamak, uncountable, countable tunggal).
- **Speaking:** gunakan pronoun untuk menghindari pengulangan: *I live with my parents. They support me a lot.*

## 7. Contoh Jawaban Model (Writing Task 1 GT, paragraf)

**Soal:** *You recently bought a laptop that stopped working. Write a letter to the shop.* Paragraf pembuka:

- **Band 5:** I buy a laptop in your shop last week. Laptop have problem. I want a refund because the laptops not work.
- **Band 6:** I bought a laptop from your shop last week, but it does not work. I would like to get a refund because the laptop has a problem.
- **Band 7:** I am writing to complain about a laptop that I purchased from your shop last Saturday. Unfortunately, it stopped working after only two days, and I would like to request a full refund.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) Artikel dan jumlah benar (*a laptop … it … the problem*), (2) pronoun *it* menghindari pengulangan, (3) kalimat lebih panjang dengan struktur jelas tanpa kehilangan akurasi.

## 8. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**
- Jamak beraturan: -s / -es / -ies / -ves. Tak beraturan: men, women, children, people, feet, teeth.
- Uncountable: information, advice, equipment, furniture, homework, news.
- a/an berdasarkan bunyi; the untuk hal spesifik; Ø untuk generalisasi.
- many/few/fewer untuk countable; much/little/less untuk uncountable.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**
1. *I need some ___ (advice) about my homework.*
2. *She is ___ (a / an) honest teacher.*
3. *There are too ___ (many / much) cars in my city, so there is a lot of traffic.*
4. *The company changed ___ (its / it's) logo.*
5. *We saw ___ (a / the) film last night. ___ (A / The) film was boring.*

**Kunci:** 1. advice (tidak berubah). 2. an. 3. many. 4. its. 5. a / The.
