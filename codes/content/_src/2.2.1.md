## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menyusun **paragraf akademik** dengan struktur **Topic Sentence + Explanation + Concrete Example (+ Link)**.
2. Menulis **topic sentence** yang jelas, fokus, dan memuat **controlling idea**.
3. Menjaga **kesatuan (unity)** dan **kohesi (coherence)**: satu ide per paragraf, urutan logis, penghubung dan kata rujukan yang tepat.
4. Menulis **contoh konkret** yang spesifik, dan memperbaiki paragraf yang lemah langkah demi langkah.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Satu paragraf = **satu ide utama**. Mulai dengan **topic sentence**, kembangkan dengan **penjelasan**, kuatkan dengan **contoh konkret**, tutup dengan **link**.
> - Rumus: **TEEL** (Topic, Explanation, Example, Link), mirip **PEEL** di Modul 3.3.
> - Contoh yang baik **spesifik** (siapa, di mana, apa), bukan "many people do this".

---

## 1. Mengapa Paragraf Penting?

Dalam Writing IELTS, **Coherence and Cohesion** dan **Task Response** (25% + 25% dari skor Task 2) sangat bergantung pada kualitas paragraf. Penguji mencari:

| Yang dicari | Tanda paragraf yang baik |
|---|---|
| **Satu ide utama** | Topic sentence jelas; semua kalimat mendukungnya |
| **Pengembangan** | Ide dijelaskan, bukan hanya disebut |
| **Contoh** | Spesifik dan relevan |
| **Urutan logis** | Dari umum ke khusus; penghubung wajar |
| **Panjang cukup** | Sekitar 80–120 kata untuk paragraf badan Task 2 |

Banyak kandidat menulis **daftar ide** (satu kalimat per ide) dan kehilangan skor karena ide tidak berkembang. Paragraf akademik menuntut **kedalaman**, bukan jumlah ide.

## 2. Anatomi Paragraf: TEEL

| Bagian | Fungsi | Panjang | Contoh (topik: olahraga di sekolah) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T**opic sentence | Menyatakan **ide utama** (controlling idea) | 1 kalimat | *Regular exercise at school can improve students' academic performance.* |
| **E**xplanation | Menjelaskan **mengapa/bagaimana** ide itu benar | 1–2 kalimat | *Physical activity increases blood flow to the brain, which helps students concentrate and remember information.* |
| **E**xample | Contoh **konkret** | 1–2 kalimat | *For instance, a student who plays football before class often feels more alert during mathematics lessons.* |
| **L**ink | Mengaitkan kembali ke thesis/pertanyaan | 1 kalimat | *Therefore, schools should include daily exercise in the timetable.* |

**Paragraf lengkap:**

*Regular exercise at school can improve students' academic performance. Physical activity increases blood flow to the brain, which helps students concentrate and remember information. For instance, a student who plays football before class often feels more alert during mathematics lessons. Therefore, schools should include daily exercise in the timetable.*

### 2.1 Peta fungsi tiap kalimat

| Kalimat | Fungsi | Penanda bahasa |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Topic | Pernyataan umum dengan controlling idea |
| 2 | Explanation | *This is because..., which means..., as...* |
| 3 | Example | *For example, For instance, A case in point is..., Take ... as an example* |
| 4 | Link | *Therefore, As a result, This shows that..., For this reason* |

:::tips-examiner
Panjang tidak sama dengan kualitas. Paragraf 90 kata yang **fokus pada satu ide** dan **dikembangkan** lebih baik daripada paragraf 150 kata berisi tiga ide dangkal. Penguji menilai **kedalaman penjelasan dan kejelasan contoh**, bukan jumlah ide.
:::

## 3. Topic Sentence yang Baik

### 3.1 Dua unsur

| Unsur | Penjelasan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic** | Hal yang dibahas | *Regular exercise at school* |
| **Controlling idea** | Pendapat/arah yang membatasi pembahasan | *can improve students' academic performance* |

**Controlling idea** menentukan **apa yang boleh dibahas** dalam paragraf. Jika topic sentence-nya *Exercise improves performance*, maka kalimat tentang "biaya peralatan olahraga" tidak relevan.

### 3.2 Topic sentence: terlalu luas, tepat, terlalu sempit

| Jenis | Contoh | Masalah |
|---|---|---|
| **Terlalu luas** | *Education is important.* | Tidak ada arah; bisa dibahas ratusan hal |
| **Tepat** | *Free education can reduce inequality by giving poor students equal opportunities.* | Fokus dan dapat dikembangkan |
| **Terlalu sempit** | *Some schools in Medan provide free textbooks in grade 3.* | Fakta kecil; tidak bisa dikembangkan jadi paragraf |
| **Fakta murni** | *Many students use laptops.* | Tidak ada argumen untuk dikembangkan |
| **Pertanyaan** | *Why is education important?* | Topic sentence sebaiknya pernyataan |

### 3.3 Pola topic sentence

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Firstly/One reason is that + klausa** | *One major reason is that online learning offers flexibility.* |
| **A key advantage of X is that...** | *A key advantage of public transport is its low cost.* |
| **X can lead to / contribute to Y** | *Excessive screen time can lead to health problems.* |
| **There are several benefits of X, the most important being...** | *There are several benefits, the most important being improved concentration.* |
| **Opinion + alasan** | *Governments should invest in railways because they reduce traffic.* |

:::kesalahan-umum
**Topic sentence yang tidak ada atau tidak jelas** adalah kesalahan paling umum. Kandidat langsung menulis detail ("In my country, there are many buses...") tanpa menyatakan **ide utamanya**. Akibatnya pembaca harus menebak maksud paragraf, dan Coherence turun. Selalu tulis ide utama di kalimat pertama.
:::

## 4. Explanation: Mengembangkan Ide

Explanation menjawab **"mengapa?"** atau **"bagaimana?"** dari topic sentence.

| Alat | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Alasan** | *This is because..., since..., as...* | *This is because students can focus better when they are physically active.* |
| **Akibat** | *which means..., so..., thus...* | *...which means they perform better in exams.* |
| **Mekanisme** | *by + V-ing, through...* | *Exercise helps by increasing blood flow to the brain.* |
| **Klarifikasi** | *In other words, That is to say...* | *In other words, a healthy body supports a healthy mind.* |
| **Perbandingan** | *unlike, compared with...* | *Compared with sitting all day, regular movement improves alertness.* |

### 4.1 Teknik "Mengapa? Lalu apa?"

| Ide awal | Mengapa? | Lalu apa? |
|---|---|---|
| Public transport reduces traffic. | Fewer people use private cars. | Air pollution and travel time decrease. |
| Homework teaches responsibility. | Students must manage their own time. | They develop discipline needed for university. |

Dua pertanyaan ini mengubah satu kalimat menjadi paragraf berisi dua sampai tiga kalimat penjelasan.

## 5. Contoh Konkret

### 5.1 Jenis contoh

| Jenis | Ciri | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Contoh umum (situasi)** | Situasi yang mudah dibayangkan | *A student who studies abroad often becomes more independent.* |
| **Contoh pribadi** | Pengalaman penulis (boleh di Task 2) | *In my own case, joining a football team improved my concentration.* |
| **Contoh hipotetis** | "Bayangkan..." | *Imagine a city with no private cars; the air would be much cleaner.* |
| **Contoh dari fakta umum** | Tren yang dikenal | *Countries such as Norway invest heavily in public transport.* |

> **Peringatan:** jangan mengarang **statistik** yang terlihat resmi (*In 2019, 87% of...*). Penguji tidak menilai keakuratan data, tetapi **angka palsu yang tidak masuk akal** terlihat tidak meyakinkan. Gunakan contoh situasi atau pernyataan umum yang wajar.

### 5.2 Contoh lemah vs. kuat

| Lemah (samar) | Kuat (spesifik) |
|---|---|
| *For example, many people use public transport.* | *For example, a commuter who takes the train to work can avoid an hour of traffic every morning.* |
| *For instance, technology is useful.* | *For instance, video calls allow a student in Medan to attend a class in Sydney.* |
| *For example, it is good for health.* | *For example, walking thirty minutes a day can lower the risk of heart disease.* |

**Rumus contoh kuat:** *Siapa/apa + melakukan apa + di mana/kapan + hasilnya.*

## 6. Link: Menutup Paragraf

| Fungsi | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Mengaitkan ke thesis** | *Therefore, ... / For this reason, ...* | *Therefore, schools should include daily exercise.* |
| **Menyimpulkan** | *This shows that..., Clearly, ...* | *This shows that physical activity supports learning.* |
| **Menyiapkan paragraf berikut** | *However, ... / Another important factor is...* | *However, exercise is not the only factor.* |

Tidak setiap paragraf butuh link, tetapi paragraf badan Task 2 sebaiknya diakhiri dengan kalimat yang **mengaitkan kembali** ke pertanyaan.

## 7. Kesatuan dan Kohesi

### 7.1 Unity (satu ide)

**Uji unity:** baca setiap kalimat dan tanyakan, *"Apakah ini mendukung topic sentence?"* Jika tidak, hapus atau pindahkan.

**Contoh paragraf dengan kalimat tidak relevan:**

*Regular exercise at school can improve students' academic performance. Physical activity increases blood flow to the brain. **School uniforms are often expensive.** For instance, a student who plays football before class feels more alert.*

Kalimat tentang seragam **tidak relevan** dan merusak unity.

### 7.2 Coherence (urutan logis)

| Cara | Penjelasan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Urutan penting** | Umum → spesifik; sebab → akibat | Topic, penjelasan, contoh |
| **Penghubung** | Menghubungkan kalimat | *Moreover, However, As a result* |
| **Kata rujukan** | *this, these, such, the latter, it* | *This increase in alertness..., Such activities...* |
| **Pengulangan kata kunci** (sewajarnya) | Menjaga topik | *exercise... physical activity...* |
| **Parallel structure** | Struktur sejajar | *It saves time, reduces cost, and protects the environment.* |

### 7.3 Kata rujukan (reference) untuk menghindari pengulangan

| Kata | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **this / these** | Merujuk ide sebelumnya | *Many students lack sleep. **This** affects their grades.* |
| **such + noun** | Merujuk jenis hal | ***Such** activities improve fitness.* |
| **the former / the latter** | Dua hal yang disebut | *Cars and trains... **The latter** is cheaper.* |
| **it / they / them** | Pronoun | *The policy was introduced. **It** reduced pollution.* |

## 8. Memperbaiki Paragraf Lemah (Langkah demi Langkah)

**Paragraf awal (Band 5):**

*Public transport is good. It is cheap. In my city there are many buses. Many people use it. Also it is good for environment.*

| Masalah | Perbaikan |
|---|---|
| Topic sentence terlalu umum (*is good*) | Tambah controlling idea |
| Penjelasan tidak ada | Jawab "mengapa murah?" |
| Contoh samar (*many people use it*) | Beri contoh spesifik |
| Tidak ada penghubung | Tambah *because, for example, as a result* |

**Langkah 1: perbaiki topic sentence.**
*Public transport offers significant economic and environmental benefits.*

**Langkah 2: tambah explanation.**
*Because many passengers share a single vehicle, the cost per person is far lower than driving a private car, and fewer cars on the road means less pollution.*

**Langkah 3: tambah contoh spesifik.**
*For example, a worker who takes the bus instead of driving can save a considerable amount of money on fuel and parking every month.*

**Langkah 4: tambah link.**
*Therefore, governments should invest in improving public transport.*

**Paragraf akhir (Band 7):**

*Public transport offers significant economic and environmental benefits. Because many passengers share a single vehicle, the cost per person is far lower than driving a private car, and fewer cars on the road means less pollution. For example, a worker who takes the bus instead of driving can save a considerable amount of money on fuel and parking every month. Therefore, governments should invest in improving public transport.*

## 9. Contoh Jawaban Model: Tiga Level Band

**Soal:** *Some people think children should learn a foreign language at primary school. Write one body paragraph supporting this view.*

- **Band 5:** Children learn language is good. They are young so fast. Many children learn. It is good for the future. Foreign language is useful.
- **Band 6:** Learning a foreign language at primary school has several advantages. Firstly, children learn quickly because their brains are still developing. For example, children who start learning English at six usually speak better than those who start later. Therefore, schools should teach languages early.
- **Band 7:** One compelling reason to introduce foreign languages in primary school is that young children absorb new sounds and vocabulary far more easily than adults. Their brains are still developing, which allows them to imitate pronunciation accurately without conscious effort. For instance, a child who attends an English-medium class from the age of six will often speak with more natural intonation than a teenager who begins later. Consequently, starting early gives learners a long-term advantage that is difficult to replicate in adulthood.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) **Topic sentence** memuat controlling idea yang jelas, (2) **explanation** menjawab "mengapa" (*which allows them to...*), (3) **contoh spesifik** (*a child who attends... will often speak...*), (4) **link** (*Consequently...*), (5) variasi penghubung dan kosakata tepat (*compelling, absorb, replicate*).

## 10. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tidak ada **topic sentence** | Kalimat pertama = ide utama | Pembaca harus menebak |
| 2 | Paragraf berisi **banyak ide** | Satu ide per paragraf | Unity |
| 3 | **Daftar** satu kalimat per ide | Kembangkan satu ide dengan penjelasan | Kedalaman |
| 4 | Contoh **samar** (*many people*) | Contoh spesifik | Tidak meyakinkan |
| 5 | **Mengarang statistik** (*87% of...*) | Contoh situasi umum | Tidak wajar |
| 6 | **Menulis ulang** topic sentence sebagai penjelasan | Penjelasan menambah informasi baru | Pengulangan |
| 7 | **Penghubung berlebihan** (*Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly* di semua paragraf) | Variasikan | Mekanis |
| 8 | Paragraf **terlalu pendek** (< 60 kata) | 80–120 kata | Kurang berkembang |
| 9 | **Kalimat tidak relevan** | Hapus | Unity |
| 10 | Tidak ada **link** ke pertanyaan | Tutup dengan kalimat penghubung | Task Response |
| 11 | **Topic sentence berupa pertanyaan** | Pernyataan | Format akademik |
| 12 | **Terlalu banyak "I think"** | Variasikan atau hapus | Gaya formal |

## 11. Strategi IELTS: Merencanakan dan Menulis Paragraf

### 11.1 Rencana 5 menit

| Langkah | Tindakan |
|---|---|
| **1. Tentukan ide utama** | Tulis kalimat (topic sentence) |
| **2. Jawab "Mengapa?"** | Catat 1–2 alasan |
| **3. Siapkan contoh** | Pilih satu situasi spesifik |
| **4. Tentukan link** | Kaitkan ke pertanyaan |

### 11.2 Daftar periksa paragraf

- [ ] Topic sentence jelas dengan controlling idea
- [ ] Setiap kalimat mendukung ide utama
- [ ] Ada penjelasan (mengapa/bagaimana)
- [ ] Ada contoh spesifik
- [ ] Ada link ke thesis
- [ ] Penghubung bervariasi
- [ ] 80–120 kata (Task 2)

### 11.3 Latihan

| Latihan | Cara | Tujuan |
|---|---|---|
| **Ubah daftar menjadi paragraf** | Ambil satu ide, tambahkan penjelasan dan contoh | Kedalaman |
| **Cari kalimat tidak relevan** | Baca paragraf contoh dan tandai | Unity |
| **Spesifikkan contoh** | Ganti contoh samar dengan situasi konkret | Kualitas contoh |
| **Tulis 5 topic sentence** untuk satu topik | Latih controlling idea | Fokus |

## 12. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**
- Paragraf = **TEEL**: Topic sentence, Explanation, Example, Link.
- Topic sentence = **topic + controlling idea**, pernyataan yang fokus.
- Explanation menjawab "mengapa/bagaimana"; contoh **spesifik**.
- Unity (satu ide), coherence (urutan logis, penghubung, kata rujukan).
- Hindari daftar ide, contoh samar, statistik karangan.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**
1. *Apa kepanjangan TEEL?*
2. *Sebutkan dua unsur topic sentence.*
3. *Mana contoh yang lebih baik: "many people use buses" atau "a worker who takes the bus saves money on fuel"?*
4. *Apa yang dimaksud unity?*
5. *Mengapa topic sentence "Education is important" lemah?*

**Kunci:** 1. Topic, Explanation, Example, Link. 2. Topic dan controlling idea. 3. Yang kedua (spesifik). 4. Semua kalimat mendukung satu ide utama. 5. Terlalu luas dan tidak punya arah yang bisa dikembangkan.
