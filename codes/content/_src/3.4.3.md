## Mulai dari Sini

:::pengantar
**Apa yang kamu pelajari?** Speaking Part 3 adalah bagian terakhir tes berbicara. Sesudah kamu bercerita di Part 2, penguji mengajakmu berdiskusi 4 sampai 5 menit tentang gagasan yang lebih luas dari topik yang sama. Kalau di Part 2 kamu bercerita tentang guru yang berpengaruh bagimu, di Part 3 pertanyaannya menjadi *What qualities does a good teacher need?* (sifat apa yang dibutuhkan guru yang baik?). Jadi yang dibahas bukan lagi dirimu, melainkan orang pada umumnya. Ini seperti beda antara ditanya "Kamu suka naik angkot?" dan "Kenapa orang kota lebih memilih kendaraan pribadi?". Halaman ini mengajarkan kerangka jawaban ARE-C (pendapat, alasan, contoh, sisi lain) dan bahasa hati-hati (*hedging*), misalnya *many people tend to...* sebagai ganti *everyone...*.

**Kenapa ini penting?** Pertanyaan Part 3 sengaja dibuat untuk menuntut analisis, jadi di sinilah kamu bisa menunjukkan ragam tata bahasa dan kosakata yang lebih tinggi. Kalau kamu menjawab satu kalimat, atau terus bercerita tentang diri sendiri, kesempatan itu hilang. Kebiasaan berpendapat dengan alasan dan menimbang sisi lain juga terpakai saat menulis esai Writing Task 2.

**Bekal sebelum mulai:** Pelajari dulu 3.4.1 (Speaking Part 1) dan 3.4.2 (Speaking Part 2), karena Part 3 melanjutkan topik kartu soal Part 2. Untuk pertanyaan pengandaian kamu perlu 2.1.4 (Conditional Sentences), dan kata penghubung dari 2.1.1 (Conjunctions & Discourse Markers) akan sering terpakai. Saran membaca: baca contoh jawaban di Bagian 5 dengan suara keras lebih dulu, lalu pakai tabel frasa di Bagian 4 sebagai rujukan saat latihan.
:::

### Istilah yang Akan Kamu Temui

| Istilah | Arti sederhana | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Abstrak** | Tentang gagasan umum, bukan pengalaman pribadi | *Why do some people stop learning after school?* |
| **ARE-C** | Kerangka jawaban: pendapat, alasan, contoh, sisi lain | *Answer, Reason, Example, Consider the other side* |
| **Hedging** | Bahasa hati-hati supaya pernyataan tidak terlalu mutlak | *tend to, might, in many cases* |
| **Speculating** | Mengandaikan hal yang belum atau tidak terjadi | *What would happen if...?* |
| **Evaluating** | Menimbang baik dan buruknya sesuatu | *Is it better to...?* |
| **Predicting** | Memperkirakan apa yang terjadi di masa depan | *Will schools exist in 50 years?* |
| **Conditional** | Kalimat pengandaian dengan *if* | *If schools had no exams, students might...* |
| **Range** | Ragam: banyaknya jenis kalimat dan kata yang kamu kuasai | memakai *if*, *whereas*, *although* |

## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menjelaskan format **Speaking Part 3** dan membedakannya dari Part 1 dan Part 2.
2. Mengenali **jenis pertanyaan abstrak**: speculating, comparing, evaluating, causes/effects, dan predicting.
3. Memakai kerangka **ARE-C: Answer + Reason + Example + Consider the other side** untuk jawaban 3–6 kalimat.
4. Memakai **bahasa hati-hati (hedging)**, conditional, dan perbandingan untuk menunjukkan **range** dan pemikiran analitis.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Part 3 = **diskusi 4–5 menit** tentang **ide umum dan abstrak** yang terkait topik Part 2.
> - Jawab bukan hanya tentang dirimu: bicaralah tentang **masyarakat, generasi, atau tren**.
> - Jawaban Band 7+ **berpendapat, beralasan, memberi contoh, dan mempertimbangkan sisi lain**, dengan bahasa yang hati-hati (*it tends to, might, in many cases*).

## 2. Jenis Pertanyaan Part 3

Pertanyaan Part 3 terdengar sulit, tetapi sebenarnya hanya meminta beberapa hal: berpendapat, mengandaikan, membandingkan, menimbang, mencari sebab dan akibat, memperkirakan masa depan, atau menggambarkan kebiasaan umum. Kalau kamu bisa mengenali jenisnya dari kata-kata awal pertanyaan, kamu langsung tahu bahasa apa yang dibutuhkan. Kolom terakhir tabel menunjukkan bahasa itu.

| Jenis | Ciri soal | Contoh | Bahasa utama |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opinion** | *Do you think...? / To what extent...?* | *Do you think teachers are respected today?* | I think..., In my opinion... |
| **Speculating** | *What would happen if...? / Might...?* | *What would happen if schools had no exams?* | would, could, might, conditional |
| **Comparing** | *How is... different from...? / Has... changed?* | *How has education changed in your country?* | whereas, compared with, used to |
| **Evaluating** | *Is it better to...? Advantages/disadvantages?* | *Is online learning better than classroom learning?* | On the one hand, however, outweigh |
| **Causes/effects** | *Why do you think...? What effect...?* | *Why do some people stop learning after school?* | because of, as a result, leads to |
| **Predicting** | *Will... in the future?* | *Will schools exist in 50 years?* | will, is likely to, probably |
| **Generalising** | *What do people usually...?* | *What do young people value in a job?* | tend to, many, generally |

:::tips-examiner
Pertanyaan Part 3 **tidak menanyakan pengalaman pribadi** secara langsung. Kandidat yang menjawab "In my case, I..." terus-menerus kehilangan kesempatan menunjukkan kemampuan analitis. Jawablah dalam tingkat **umum** (*many people, young people, in many countries*), lalu bila perlu tambahkan satu contoh pribadi atau umum sebagai pendukung.
:::

## 3. Kerangka Jawaban ARE-C

Di Part 1 kamu memakai rumus DRE (jawab, alasan, contoh). ARE-C adalah versi yang lebih panjang untuk Part 3: tiga langkah yang sama, ditambah satu langkah baru, yaitu mengakui sisi lain dari pendapatmu. Langkah tambahan ini membuat jawabanmu lebih berimbang. Diagram dan tabel di bawah menerapkannya pada satu pertanyaan.

:::flow
Answer | Pendapat langsung
Reason | Alasan utama
Example | Contoh konkret
Consider the other side | Sisi lain atau kualifikasi
:::

| Langkah | Fungsi | Contoh (pertanyaan: *Is online learning better than classroom learning?*) |
|---|---|---|
| **A**nswer | Posisi/pendapat langsung | *It depends on the learner, but I'd say classroom learning is generally more effective.* |
| **R**eason | Alasan utama | *That's because students can ask questions immediately and stay more focused.* |
| **E**xample | Contoh konkret | *For example, in my experience, group discussions work much better face to face.* |
| **C**onsider the other side | Sisi lain / kualifikasi | *That said, online learning is more flexible, so it suits people who work full time.* |

**Jawaban lengkap:** *It depends on the learner, but I'd say classroom learning is generally more effective. That's because students can ask questions immediately and stay more focused. For example, group discussions work much better face to face. That said, online learning is more flexible, so it suits people who work full time.*

#### Variasi pembuka jawaban

| Tujuan | Frasa |
|---|---|
| Berpendapat | I'd say..., I tend to think..., As far as I'm concerned..., Personally, I believe... |
| Mengakui kerumitan | It's a tricky question, but..., It depends on..., There are two sides to this. |
| Waktu berpikir | That's an interesting question. Let me think..., Well, ... |
| Generalisasi | Generally speaking..., In many cases..., For the most part... |

## 4. Bahasa untuk Tiap Jenis

Anggap bagian ini sebagai kotak perkakas. Untuk jenis-jenis pertanyaan dari Bagian 2, ada tabel kecil berisi pola kalimat dan contohnya. Mulailah dari 4.1, yaitu bahasa hati-hati, karena bisa dipakai untuk jenis pertanyaan apa pun. Tabel lainnya cukup kamu buka saat berlatih jenis pertanyaan yang bersangkutan.

### 4.1 Hedging (bahasa hati-hati)

| Hindari (mutlak) | Pakai (hati-hati) |
|---|---|
| Everyone thinks that... | **Many people** think that... |
| Technology is bad for children. | Technology **can be** harmful if children use it **excessively**. |
| It will definitely happen. | It's **likely** to happen. / It **might** happen. |
| Young people never read. | Young people **tend to** read less than before. |

**Kata hedging:** *may, might, could, tend to, generally, often, in many cases, to some extent, it seems that, it's likely that.*

### 4.2 Speculating (conditional)

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **If + Past Simple, would + V1** | *If schools had no exams, students **might** focus more on real understanding.* |
| **might / could + V1** | *It **could** reduce stress, but it **might** also lower motivation.* |
| **Should / ought to** (saran) | *Governments **should** invest more in public transport.* |

Lihat Modul 2.1.4 untuk rumus conditional.

### 4.3 Comparing

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **compared with / than** | *Compared with 20 years ago, people travel much more.* |
| **whereas / while** | *Older people prefer print, **whereas** younger people read online.* |
| **used to / these days** | *People **used to** write letters, but **these days** they send messages.* |
| **much more / far less / slightly** | *Education is **far more accessible** now.* |
| **the gap between** | *The gap between rich and poor has widened.* |

### 4.4 Evaluating

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **On the one hand..., on the other hand...** | *On the one hand, it saves time; on the other hand, it reduces personal contact.* |
| **The main advantage/drawback is...** | *The main drawback is that it can be expensive.* |
| **outweigh** | *In my view, the benefits outweigh the risks.* |
| **It depends on...** | *It depends on how it's used.* |

### 4.5 Causes dan effects

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **because of / due to** | *Because of long working hours, many people have little free time.* |
| **lead to / result in / contribute to** | *Stress can **lead to** health problems.* |
| **as a result / consequently** | *Prices rose. **As a result**, fewer people bought cars.* |
| **the reason (why) is that...** | *The reason many people move is that cities offer more jobs.* |

### 4.6 Predicting

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **will / won't / probably** | *I think it will probably become more common.* |
| **is likely to / is expected to** | *Remote work is likely to continue.* |
| **by then / in the long run** | *In the long run, schools may rely more on technology.* |

## 5. Contoh Jawaban Model: Pertanyaan dan Jawaban Band 7

Berikut lima pertanyaan dari lima jenis yang berbeda, lengkap dengan jawaban tingkat Band 7. Panjangnya hanya dua sampai empat kalimat, jadi kamu bisa melihat bahwa jawaban yang baik tidak harus panjang. Baris "Alat" di bawah tiap jawaban menyebut kata atau pola yang layak kamu tiru.

### 5.1 Speculating

**Pertanyaan:** *What would happen if everyone worked from home?*

*If everyone worked from home, cities would probably be much quieter, and commuters would save a great deal of time and money. On the other hand, many people might feel isolated, and companies would have to find new ways to keep their teams connected.*

**Alat:** conditional, hedging, dua sisi.

### 5.2 Comparing

**Pertanyaan:** *How has the way people communicate changed over the past few decades?*

*Compared with the past, communication has become much faster and more informal. People used to write letters or call from landlines, whereas these days most conversations happen through messaging apps. That said, I think something has been lost: face-to-face conversations seem to be less common among younger people.*

**Alat:** compared with, used to, whereas, that said.

### 5.3 Evaluating

**Pertanyaan:** *Is it better to live in a city or in the countryside?*

*It really depends on the stage of life. For young people, cities offer more jobs and entertainment, which is a major advantage. However, the countryside tends to be cheaper and quieter, so it suits families or people who value a slower pace. On balance, I'd say the benefits of city life outweigh the drawbacks when you're starting your career.*

**Alat:** it depends, however, tends to, on balance.

### 5.4 Causes dan effects

**Pertanyaan:** *Why do some people find it hard to learn a foreign language?*

*There are probably several reasons. Many adults have little time to practise, and they often feel embarrassed about making mistakes. As a result, they tend to give up after a few months. In addition, if they have no chance to use the language in daily life, progress can be very slow.*

**Alat:** probably, as a result, tend to, if.

### 5.5 Predicting

**Pertanyaan:** *Do you think books will disappear in the future?*

*I don't think they'll disappear completely, but they might become less common. E-books are already popular because they're cheap and convenient. Even so, many people still prefer the feel of a printed book, so I'd expect both formats to exist side by side for a long time.*

**Alat:** I don't think..., might, even so, I'd expect.

## 6. Perbandingan Tiga Level Band

Di sini satu pertanyaan tentang teknologi dijawab dalam tiga tingkat kemampuan. Ketiga jawaban sama-sama menyebut sisi baik dan sisi buruk teknologi. Yang berbeda adalah cara menyampaikannya. Coba sebutkan sendiri apa yang bertambah di tiap tingkat sebelum membaca penjelasan di bawah daftar.

**Pertanyaan:** *Do you think technology has made people's lives better?*

- **Band 5:** Yes, technology is good. People can use phone. It is easy. But sometimes it is bad for children.
- **Band 6:** I think technology has made our lives better because we can communicate easily and find information quickly. However, some people spend too much time on their phones, which is not healthy.
- **Band 7:** On balance, I'd say it has, although it's a double-edged sword. On the one hand, we can communicate instantly and access information that used to take hours to find. On the other hand, many people, especially young people, tend to spend so much time online that they neglect real-life relationships. So the benefits are clear, but they depend on how responsibly the technology is used.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) **Posisi + kualifikasi** (*On balance, although*), (2) **dua sisi** (*On the one hand..., On the other hand*), (3) **hedging** (*tend to, many people, depend on*), (4) **bahasa abstrak** (*double-edged sword, neglect*), (5) tidak berhenti pada jawaban satu kalimat.

## 7. Menghadapi Pertanyaan Sulit

Cepat atau lambat kamu akan mendapat pertanyaan tentang hal yang belum pernah kamu pikirkan. Itu wajar dan tidak perlu membuatmu panik. Tabel di bawah memberi satu jalan keluar untuk tiap situasi, lengkap dengan kalimat yang bisa langsung dipakai. Di bawahnya ada daftar pasangan kontras sebagai sumber ide saat pikiranmu kosong.

| Situasi | Apa yang dilakukan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Pertanyaan tidak dipahami** | Minta pengulangan (satu kali) | *Sorry, could you repeat that, please?* |
| **Topik tidak dikenal** | Jawab secara umum, dari logika | *I haven't thought about this much, but I'd imagine that...* |
| **Tidak punya pendapat** | Beri pandangan sederhana dengan alasan | *I'm not sure, but I'd say..., because...* |
| **Butuh waktu** | Frasa pengisi | *That's a good question. Let me think...* |
| **Kehabisan ide** | Pakai tiga sumber: kelompok orang, waktu, dampak | *For older people..., In the future..., As a result...* |
| **Lupa kata** | Parafrase | *a system where..., the thing people use to...* |

**Sumber ide yang selalu ada:** *young people vs. older people, rich vs. poor, cities vs. countryside, past vs. present, individuals vs. society, advantages vs. disadvantages.*

:::kesalahan-umum
**Jawaban satu-dua kalimat tanpa alasan** adalah kesalahan terbesar di Part 3. *"Yes, I think it is good."* tidak cukup. Penguji sengaja mengajukan pertanyaan yang menuntut analisis; jika kamu berhenti di jawaban pertama, kamu kehilangan kesempatan menunjukkan **Grammatical Range** dan **Lexical Resource** Band 7. Setelah jawabanmu, selalu tambahkan **alasan** dan **contoh atau sisi lain**.
:::

## 9. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

Tabel ini mengumpulkan kesalahan yang sering muncul di Part 3. Sebagian menyangkut isi jawaban, seperti terlalu pendek atau hanya membahas diri sendiri. Sebagian lagi menyangkut bahasa, seperti *I am agree*. Rekam satu jawaban latihanmu, dengarkan ulang, lalu cek terhadap tiap baris tabel.

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jawaban **satu kalimat** | Tambah alasan dan contoh | Tidak menunjukkan range |
| 2 | Hanya bicara tentang **diri sendiri** | Bicara tentang **orang pada umumnya** | Part 3 bersifat abstrak |
| 3 | **Kata mutlak** (*everyone, always, never*) | *many, tend to, often* | Hedging |
| 4 | **I am agree** with that. | **I agree** with that. | *Agree* sudah kata kerja |
| 5 | **Because ..., so ...** dalam satu kalimat | Pilih satu | Penghubung ganda |
| 6 | Tidak memakai **conditional** untuk hipotesis | *If..., would...* | Range grammar |
| 7 | **Menghafal ungkapan** tidak sesuai konteks | Gunakan yang relevan | Tidak natural |
| 8 | **Diam lama** saat berpikir | *That's an interesting question...* | Fluency |
| 9 | Hanya **sisi satu** | *On the other hand, ...* | Analisis |
| 10 | **Mengulang pertanyaan** persis sebagai jawaban | Parafrase dan jawab | Lexical |
| 11 | **Bicara terlalu panjang** (>1 menit) | 3–6 kalimat | Penguji perlu bertanya lagi |
| 12 | Terlalu **informal** (*stuff, kind of, you know*) | *various things, to some extent* | Register |

## 10. Strategi IELTS: Persiapan dan Hari Tes

Kemampuan berdiskusi tidak datang dari menghafal jawaban, tetapi dari kebiasaan berpendapat dengan alasan. Bagian ini memberi latihan harian yang bisa kamu lakukan sendiri dengan perekam suara, lalu beberapa pegangan untuk saat tes berlangsung.

### 10.1 Latihan sehari-hari

| Latihan | Cara | Tujuan |
|---|---|---|
| **Jawab 3 pertanyaan abstrak** dengan ARE-C, direkam | Pilih topik berbeda | Struktur dan analisis |
| **Ganti jawaban satu kalimat** dengan jawaban 4 kalimat | Tambah reason, example, other side | Mengembangkan ide |
| **Latih hedging** | Ubah kalimat mutlak menjadi hati-hati | Akurasi dan nuansa |
| **Baca/dengarkan** diskusi (podcast, berita) | Catat frasa untuk berpendapat | Kosakata dan struktur |
| **Latih 5 pasangan kontras** | *young/old, city/countryside, past/present...* | Sumber ide cepat |

### 10.2 Saat tes

- **Dengarkan jenis pertanyaan** (apakah spekulasi, perbandingan, evaluasi?) lalu pilih alat bahasanya.
- **Jawab langsung**, lalu **kembangkan** dengan alasan dan contoh.
- **Bicaralah secara umum**, bukan hanya pengalamanmu.
- **Jangan takut berubah pikiran**: *On second thought...* menunjukkan kemampuan.
- **Pertahankan nada** percakapan; tidak perlu kata yang rumit.

## 11. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

Lima butir "Ingat" di bawah merangkum halaman ini. Sesudah membacanya, jawab pertanyaan Cek Diri tanpa melihat materi, lalu cocokkan dengan kunci. Kalau nomor 3 atau 4 masih meleset, buka lagi Bagian 4.1 dan 4.2 sebelum mengerjakan latihan.

**Ingat:**

- Part 3: diskusi abstrak 4–5 menit; jawab **3–6 kalimat**, tingkat **umum**.
- Kerangka **ARE-C**: Answer, Reason, Example, Consider other side.
- Bahasa: hedging, conditional (speculating), comparatives, *on the one hand...*, *lead to*.
- Kata mutlak berbahaya; gunakan *many, tend to, often, might*.
- Jangan berhenti di satu kalimat.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *Apa kepanjangan ARE-C?*
2. *Berapa kalimat ideal untuk jawaban Part 3?*
3. *Ubah ke hedging: "Everyone uses social media."*
4. *Pola conditional apa untuk pertanyaan "What would happen if...?"*
5. *Mengapa jawaban satu kalimat berisiko?*

**Kunci:** 1. Answer, Reason, Example, Consider the other side. 2. 3–6 kalimat. 3. *Many people use social media.* / *Social media is widely used.* 4. If + Past Simple, would + V1. 5. Tidak menunjukkan range tata bahasa dan kosakata.
