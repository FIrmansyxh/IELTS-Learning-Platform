## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Membangun **kohesi** tanpa bergantung pada linking words yang kaku (*Firstly, Secondly, Moreover* di setiap kalimat).
2. Memakai **kata rujukan**, **substitusi**, **rantai leksikal** (sinonim dan kata terkait), dan **ringkasan noun** (*this trend, such measures*) untuk menghubungkan ide.
3. Menyusun **informasi lama-baru** (given-new) sehingga paragraf mengalir secara logis.
4. Memakai **tanda baca dan struktur kalimat** (titik koma, titik dua, klausa kontras) sebagai penghubung alami.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - **Cohesion** = tautan antar-kalimat (kata rujukan, sinonim, struktur); **coherence** = logika keseluruhan.
> - Band 7+ membangun kohesi lewat **bahasa**, bukan daftar penghubung. *The government banned plastic bags. **This measure** reduced waste.* (tanpa *Moreover*).
> - Satu penghubung tepat > tiga yang dipaksakan. Tujuannya mengalir, bukan menghias.

## 1. Kohesi vs. Koherensi

| | **Cohesion** | **Coherence** |
|---|---|---|
| **Arti** | Alat bahasa yang menghubungkan kalimat | Logika dan kejelasan keseluruhan |
| **Contoh alat** | *this, such, however, it, synonyms* | Urutan ide, satu ide per paragraf |
| **Penguji menilai** | Tautan kalimat tampak natural | Pembaca mudah mengikuti argumen |

**Coherence and Cohesion** bernilai **25%** dari skor Writing. Band 7 menuntut **pengaturan informasi yang logis** dan **berbagai alat kohesi yang tepat**, bukan sekadar daftar penghubung.

## 2. Mengapa Linking Words yang Kaku Merugikan?

| Pola yang mekanis | Masalah |
|---|---|
| *Firstly..., Secondly..., Thirdly...* di setiap paragraf | Terlihat dihafal |
| *Moreover, Furthermore, In addition* di setiap kalimat | Berlebihan, kehilangan makna |
| *However* di awal dua kalimat berturut-turut | Tidak ada logika kontras |
| Penghubung tidak sesuai logika (*Therefore* tanpa sebab) | Mengganggu koherensi |

**Contoh mekanis (Band 6):**
*Firstly, public transport is cheap. Moreover, it reduces traffic. Furthermore, it protects the environment. In conclusion, public transport is good.*

**Versi dengan kohesi alami (Band 7):**
*Public transport is considerably cheaper than driving, which makes it accessible to low-income workers. **This affordability** encourages more people to leave their cars at home, **thereby** reducing congestion and **the pollution it causes**.*

## 3. Alat Kohesi Selain Linking Words

### 3.1 Kata rujukan (reference)

| Alat | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **this / these** | Merujuk ide sebelumnya | Many students lack sleep. **This** affects their grades. |
| **such + noun** | Merujuk jenis hal | Governments introduced new taxes. **Such measures** are controversial. |
| **it / they / them** | Pronoun | The policy was introduced in 2015. **It** has since been revised. |
| **the former / the latter** | Dua hal yang disebut | Cars and trains... **The latter** is cheaper. |
| **the same / another / other** | Hubungan | Cities face **the same** problem. |
| **here / there / then** | Tempat dan waktu | In Jakarta, **there** are many cars. |

### 3.2 Ringkasan noun (summarising nouns)

Satu noun merangkum ide kalimat sebelumnya. **Sangat efektif untuk kohesi akademik.**

| Noun ringkasan | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **this trend / this shift / this development** | Fewer people read print newspapers. **This trend** shows no sign of reversing. |
| **this approach / this strategy / this policy** | The council banned cars in the centre. **This approach** has reduced noise. |
| **such measures / such practices** | **Such practices** are common in many countries. |
| **this phenomenon / this issue / this problem** | Obesity is rising. **This issue** places pressure on hospitals. |
| **these findings / these results** | **These findings** suggest a clear link. |
| **this decision / this change** | **This change** met strong opposition. |

### 3.3 Substitusi dan elipsis

| Alat | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Substitusi** (*one, do, so, the same*) | *Some prefer cars; **others** prefer trains.* |
| **Elipsis** (menghilangkan kata yang sudah jelas) | *Some students study in libraries, others [study] at home.* |
| **do so** | *Governments should act, and many have **done so**.* |

### 3.4 Rantai leksikal (lexical chains)

Gunakan **sinonim, kata terkait, dan word family** agar topik terhubung tanpa pengulangan kata persis.

| Topik | Rantai leksikal |
|---|---|
| **Pollution** | pollution → emissions → smog → air quality → environmental damage |
| **Education** | education → learning → schooling → curriculum → academic performance |
| **Unemployment** | unemployment → job losses → redundancy → the labour market |

### 3.5 Pengulangan strategis

Mengulang **kata kunci** sesekali menjaga fokus: *Public transport reduces traffic. **Public transport** also lowers costs.* Tetapi variasikan setelah dua kali.

:::tips-examiner
Cara cepat memeriksa kohesi: **garis bawahi semua penghubung di paragrafmu**. Jika hampir setiap kalimat dimulai dengan satu, ganti sebagian dengan **kata rujukan** (*this, such*), **ringkasan noun** (*this trend*), atau **struktur kalimat** (*While X..., Y...*). Kohesi terbaik **tidak terasa**; pembaca merasa mengalir.
:::

## 4. Informasi Lama dan Baru (Given-New)

Susun kalimat agar **informasi lama (sudah diketahui)** ada di **awal**, **informasi baru** di **akhir**. Kalimat berikutnya memulai dengan **informasi baru** dari kalimat sebelumnya.

| Kalimat | Lama | Baru |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Many cities have introduced **congestion charges**. | cities | congestion charges |
| 2. **These charges** reduce traffic, but **they** also raise costs for small businesses. | These charges | reduce traffic, raise costs |
| 3. **The cost issue** has led to calls for exemptions. | The cost issue | calls for exemptions |

**Pola:** akhir kalimat n → awal kalimat n+1 (**rantai**).

## 5. Kohesi lewat Struktur Kalimat

### 5.1 Klausa kontras dan konsesi (tanpa *However* di awal)

| Dengan penghubung | Dengan struktur |
|---|---|
| Cars are convenient. **However**, they pollute. | **While** cars are convenient, they pollute. |
| Cities offer jobs. **However**, housing is expensive. | **Although** cities offer jobs, housing is expensive. |
| Public transport is cheap. **On the other hand**, it is slow. | Public transport is cheap, **but** slow. |

### 5.2 Sebab dan akibat dengan struktur

| Dengan penghubung | Dengan struktur |
|---|---|
| Prices rose. **As a result**, demand fell. | Prices rose, **which** reduced demand. / **Rising prices led to** falling demand. |
| Technology improved. **Therefore**, productivity increased. | **Improved technology boosted** productivity. |

### 5.3 Tanda baca sebagai penghubung

| Tanda | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Titik koma ( ; )** | Menghubungkan dua klausa terkait | Cars are convenient; **public transport is cheaper**. |
| **Titik dua ( : )** | Penjelasan/daftar | There are two main causes: **poverty and poor education**. |
| **Tanda pisah ( — )** | Menambahkan penjelasan | The policy — **widely criticised** — was withdrawn. |
| **Koma + participle** | Hasil/aksi lanjutan | Prices fell, **leading to** higher demand. |

### 5.4 Transisi antar paragraf

| Teknik | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Ringkasan noun + pembuka baru** | *This reliance on private cars has wider consequences for health.* |
| **Kontras halus** | *Despite these benefits, critics point to several drawbacks.* |
| **Pertanyaan retoris (jarang)** | *But what are the long-term effects?* |
| **Sudut pandang baru** | *From an economic perspective, ...* |

## 6. Variasi Contoh: Dari Mekanis ke Kohesif

**Level 1: dasar**
1. *Many people drive. This causes traffic.* → ***This** causes traffic.* (rujukan)
2. *Some prefer cars. Some prefer trains.* → *Some prefer cars; **others** prefer trains.* (substitusi)

**Level 2: ringkasan noun**
3. *The government raised taxes on sugary drinks. This measure reduced consumption.*
4. *Obesity is increasing. This trend places pressure on hospitals.*

**Level 3: struktur dan lexical chain**
5. *Although cars are convenient, they contribute significantly to air pollution; this raises serious concerns about public health.*
6. *Rapid urbanisation strains public services. Such strain is most visible in housing, where demand far exceeds supply.*
7. *Public transport reduces congestion. Fewer vehicles on the road, in turn, mean lower emissions, which improves air quality.*

## 7. Contoh Jawaban Model: Tiga Level Band

**Soal:** *Some people think governments should invest in public transport. Write one body paragraph.*

- **Band 5:** Public transport is good. It is cheap. Also it is good for environment. Then traffic is less. Also people can save money.
- **Band 6:** Firstly, public transport is cheaper than cars. Moreover, it reduces traffic congestion. Furthermore, it is better for the environment. Therefore, governments should invest in it.
- **Band 7:** Public transport is considerably cheaper than driving, which makes it accessible to workers on low incomes. This affordability encourages more people to leave their cars at home, thereby reducing congestion and the pollution it causes. Such benefits, moreover, extend beyond individuals, since cleaner air lowers healthcare costs for the whole community. For these reasons, governments should treat public transport as a priority.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) **Ringkasan noun** (*This affordability, Such benefits*), (2) **rujukan** (*it, the pollution it causes*), (3) **struktur** (*which makes..., thereby reducing...*), (4) **lexical chain** (*cheaper, affordability, healthcare costs*), (5) satu *moreover* yang bermakna, bukan penghubung di setiap kalimat.

## 8. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

:::kesalahan-umum
Dua kesalahan kohesi paling umum: **(1) penghubung mekanis** (*Firstly, Moreover, Furthermore* di setiap kalimat) dan **(2) rujukan yang ambigu** (*This is bad.* tanpa noun yang jelas). Perbaiki dengan **ringkasan noun** (*This trend is harmful.*) dan struktur kalimat (*While..., ...*), bukan dengan menambah penghubung lagi.
:::

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly* di setiap paragraf | Variasikan atau hilangkan | Mekanis |
| 2 | *Moreover* di setiap kalimat | Gunakan rujukan dan struktur | Berlebihan |
| 3 | *This* **tanpa noun** yang jelas (*This is bad.*) | *This trend is harmful.* | Ambigu |
| 4 | Pronoun **ambigu** (*It* merujuk apa?) | Ganti dengan noun | Kejelasan |
| 5 | **Mengulang kata sama** terus | Sinonim dan lexical chain | Lexical Resource |
| 6 | Penghubung **salah logika** | Pilih yang sesuai makna | Koherensi |
| 7 | **Comma splice** | Titik koma/konjungsi | Tata bahasa |
| 8 | Kalimat **tanpa tautan** (daftar ide) | Rangkai dengan rujukan | Cohesion |
| 9 | **However** untuk menambah ide | *In addition, Moreover* | Makna |
| 10 | **Paragraf tanpa topic sentence** | Tulis ide utama | Lihat 2.2.1 |
| 11 | **Pembuka paragraf** tanpa transisi | Ringkasan noun/kontras | Alur |
| 12 | Menghafal **kalimat transisi** | Sesuaikan isi | Natural |

## 9. Strategi IELTS: Cara Berlatih dan Memakai

### 9.1 Latihan sehari-hari

| Latihan | Cara |
|---|---|
| **Ubah paragraf mekanis** | Ganti setengah penghubung dengan rujukan dan ringkasan noun |
| **Cari penghubung** | Garis bawahi penghubung di esai lamamu; kurangi |
| **Latihan ringkasan noun** | Untuk 10 kalimat, tulis noun yang merangkumnya (*this trend, this measure*) |
| **Susun given-new** | Tulis tiga kalimat yang saling menyambung lewat akhir-awal |
| **Baca teks akademik** | Tandai alat kohesi yang dipakai penulis |

### 9.2 Saat tes

- **Writing:** pakai **dua sampai tiga ringkasan noun** per esai, **dua struktur kontras** (*While..., Although...*), dan **satu atau dua penghubung** yang bermakna.
- **Cek akhir:** tiap *this/it* jelas merujuk pada apa? Ada paragraf yang terlalu banyak penghubung?
- **Speaking:** gunakan *That's why..., which means..., So...* secara natural.

## 10. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**
- Kohesi dibangun lewat **rujukan, ringkasan noun, substitusi, lexical chain, struktur kalimat, dan tanda baca**, bukan hanya penghubung.
- Susun **informasi lama → baru**; akhir kalimat → awal kalimat berikutnya.
- Hindari *Firstly... Secondly...* mekanis dan *Moreover* di setiap kalimat.
- Pastikan *this/it/they* jelas merujuk pada apa.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**
1. *Ganti "Moreover" dengan ringkasan noun: "Fewer people read newspapers. ___ shows no sign of reversing."*
2. *Mengapa "This is bad." bisa lemah?*
3. *Ubah ke struktur: "Cars are convenient. However, they pollute."*
4. *Apa fungsi titik koma?*
5. *Apa risiko Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly di semua paragraf?*

**Kunci:** 1. This trend. 2. "This" tidak merujuk pada noun yang jelas (ambigu). 3. While cars are convenient, they pollute. 4. Menghubungkan dua klausa yang berkaitan. 5. Terlihat mekanis dan dihafal.
