## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menjelaskan format **Speaking Part 2** (cue card, 1 menit persiapan, bicara 1–2 menit).
2. Membuat **catatan 1 menit** yang efektif: kata kunci, bukan kalimat.
3. Menyusun jawaban dengan **struktur cerita** (pembuka, detail, perasaan, penutup) dan **mengisi waktu** hingga 2 menit secara natural.
4. Memakai **tense** dan **kosakata deskriptif** yang tepat untuk berbagai tipe cue card.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Kamu diberi **cue card**, **1 menit** untuk mencatat, lalu bicara **1–2 menit** tanpa interupsi.
> - Catat **kata kunci** untuk keempat poin kartu, lalu bicara mengikuti urutannya.
> - **Jangan berhenti sebelum 1,5 menit**. Waktu yang terlalu singkat menurunkan Fluency dan menandakan ide kurang berkembang.

## 1. Format Part 2

| Tahap | Waktu | Apa yang terjadi |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Instruksi** | ± 10 detik | Penguji memberikan cue card, kertas, dan pensil |
| **2. Persiapan** | **1 menit** | Kamu mencatat; penguji diam |
| **3. Monolog** | **1–2 menit** | Kamu berbicara; penguji menghentikan setelah 2 menit |
| **4. Pertanyaan lanjutan** | ± 30 detik | Penguji mengajukan 1–2 pertanyaan singkat (*rounding off*) |

### 1.1 Bentuk cue card

> **Describe a useful skill that you learned.**
> You should say:
> - what the skill is
> - when and how you learned it
> - who taught you
> and explain why you think this skill is useful.

| Bagian kartu | Fungsi |
|---|---|
| **Describe a/an ...** | Topik utama |
| **You should say** + 3 poin | Panduan isi; bukan daftar wajib berurutan, tetapi lebih baik **dicakup semua** |
| **and explain why...** | Poin keempat: **alasan, pendapat, atau dampak**; sering dilewati kandidat padahal penting |

## 2. Tipe Topik Cue Card dan Tense

| Tipe topik | Contoh | Tense utama |
|---|---|---|
| **Orang** | A person who influenced you | Present (siapa dia) + Past (kejadian) |
| **Tempat** | A place you visited | Past Simple + Present (deskripsi) |
| **Benda** | An object you value | Present Simple + Past (asal-usul) |
| **Kejadian/pengalaman** | A memorable event | **Past Simple** + Past Continuous |
| **Aktivitas/kegiatan** | A hobby or skill | Present Simple + Past Simple |
| **Rencana/masa depan** | Something you want to do | will / going to / would |

Tetap konsisten: jika topik masa lalu, jangan melompat ke present tanpa alasan.

## 3. Persiapan 1 Menit: Catatan Efektif

### 3.1 Prinsip

| Lakukan | Hindari |
|---|---|
| Tulis **kata kunci** (1–3 kata) | Menulis kalimat lengkap |
| Satu baris per poin kartu | Membuang waktu menulis rapi |
| **Tambahkan** 1–2 detail per poin (contoh, perasaan) | Hanya menyalin kartu |
| Tulis **kata kosakata** yang ingin dipakai | Menghafal naskah |

### 3.2 Contoh catatan (cue card: *a useful skill you learned*)

| Poin kartu | Catatan (kata kunci) |
|---|---|
| **What** | cooking, simple meals, rice + egg |
| **When/how** | age 15, mom taught, weekends, YouTube |
| **Who** | mom (patient, funny) |
| **Why useful** | save money, healthy, can help family, **fun** |

**Aturan waktu catatan:** 15 detik per poin; sisakan 5 detik untuk menyiapkan kalimat pembuka.

### 3.3 Struktur cerita 4 bagian

| Bagian | Isi | Waktu |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Pembuka** | Jawab topik langsung: *I'd like to talk about...* | 10 detik |
| **2. Detail** | Poin 1–3 kartu dengan detail | 60–70 detik |
| **3. Alasan / perasaan** | *Explain why*: dampak, pendapat | 30–40 detik |
| **4. Penutup** | Kalimat penutup singkat | 5–10 detik |

:::tips-examiner
**Jangan berhenti di bawah 1,5 menit**. Jika penguji belum menghentikanmu dan kamu kehabisan ide, tambahkan satu **detail panca indera** (apa yang kamu lihat, dengar, rasakan), **perasaan**, atau **perbandingan** (*It's different from..., compared with...*). Dan jangan khawatir bila penguji memotongmu di 2 menit; itu normal dan bukan tanda kesalahan.
:::

## 4. Teknik Mengisi Waktu Secara Natural

| Teknik | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Detail indra** | *I could see/hear/smell...* | *The kitchen smelled of garlic and fried rice.* |
| **Perasaan** | *I felt..., it made me feel...* | *I felt proud when everyone finished the meal.* |
| **Alasan lebih dalam** | *The reason is that..., That's because...* | *That's because cooking taught me patience.* |
| **Perbandingan** | *Compared with..., unlike...* | *Unlike eating out, I know exactly what goes into my food.* |
| **Dampak / hasil** | *As a result, ... Since then...* | *Since then, I've cooked for my family every Sunday.* |
| **Pendapat dan refleksi** | *Looking back..., if I could change...* | *Looking back, I wish I had started earlier.* |
| **Contoh/anekdot** | *For example, ... One time...* | *One time, I burned the rice, but we laughed about it.* |
| **Masa depan** | *I'd like to..., I'm planning to...* | *I'd like to learn to bake next.* |

### 4.1 Frasa transisi untuk monolog

| Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Membuka** | I'd like to talk about..., Let me tell you about..., The skill I'd like to describe is... |
| **Beralih ke poin berikut** | As for..., Moving on to..., When it comes to... |
| **Menambah** | On top of that..., What's more..., Another thing is... |
| **Alasan** | The main reason is..., Mainly because... |
| **Menutup** | All in all, ..., To sum up, ..., That's why I think... |

## 5. Tata Bahasa dan Kosakata untuk Part 2

### 5.1 Struktur yang menaikkan skor

| Struktur | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Relative clause** | *My mother, who is a great cook, taught me.* |
| **Past Continuous + Past Simple** | *While I was waiting, I met an old friend.* |
| **Past Perfect** | *It was the first time I had cooked alone.* |
| **Conditional (third)** | *If I hadn't learned, I wouldn't have been able to help.* |
| **Used to / would** | *We would cook together every Saturday.* |
| **Penghubung kontras** | *Although it was difficult at first, ...* |

### 5.2 Kosakata deskriptif

| Kategori | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Orang** | patient, supportive, easy-going, hard-working, a role model |
| **Tempat** | peaceful, crowded, breathtaking, vibrant, off the beaten track |
| **Benda** | sentimental value, a gift from..., handmade, durable |
| **Perasaan** | thrilled, proud, nervous, relieved, overwhelmed |
| **Pengalaman** | unforgettable, a turning point, it taught me..., it opened my eyes |

## 6. Contoh Jawaban Model: Catatan dan Jawaban Lengkap

**Cue card:** *Describe a useful skill that you learned.*

### 6.1 Catatan
cooking | age 15, mom, weekends | patient, funny | save money, healthy, fun

### 6.2 Jawaban Band 7 (± 230 kata, sekitar 1 menit 50 detik)

*I'd like to talk about cooking, which is a skill I picked up when I was about fifteen. Before that, I could hardly boil an egg, so my mother decided it was time for me to learn.*

*She taught me on weekends, usually in the afternoon when the kitchen was quiet. We started with really simple dishes, like fried rice with an egg, and she was incredibly patient, even when I burned the rice more than once. I also watched a lot of cooking videos on YouTube to pick up new ideas, so it was a mixture of learning from her and teaching myself.*

*What I remember most is how funny those afternoons were. My mum has a great sense of humour, and she would tease me whenever I added too much salt. Looking back, those mistakes were actually the best part of learning, because I never forgot them.*

*I think it's useful for several reasons. First, it saves money, since eating at home is much cheaper than eating out. Second, it's healthier because I control exactly what goes into my food. And finally, it's a way of caring for people: these days I cook for my family every Sunday, and seeing them enjoy the meal makes me feel really proud.*

*All in all, it's one of the most valuable things I've ever learned, and I'd love to try baking next.*

**Analisis (mengapa Band 7):**

| Aspek | Contoh dalam jawaban |
|---|---|
| **Fluency** | Mengalir, penghubung natural (*Before that, What I remember most, All in all*) |
| **Struktur** | Pembuka, detail, alasan, penutup |
| **Grammar** | Past Simple, Past Continuous (*was quiet*), relative clause (*which is a skill*) |
| **Lexical** | *picked up, incredibly patient, a great sense of humour, a way of caring* |
| **Poin "explain why"** | Dijawab dengan tiga alasan (hemat, sehat, peduli) |

## 7. Perbandingan Tiga Level Band (pembuka dan detail)

**Cue card:** *Describe a place you visited.*

- **Band 5:** I go to Bali. It is beautiful. I go with my family. I like beach. Food is good. Is very nice.
- **Band 6:** I'd like to talk about Bali, which I visited with my family last year. It was a beautiful place, and we spent most of our time at the beach. I really liked the food, and the people were friendly.
- **Band 7:** I'd like to talk about Bali, which I visited with my family last summer. We stayed in a small guesthouse near the beach, and every evening we'd walk along the shore to watch the sunset. What struck me most was how welcoming the locals were, which made the trip feel much more personal than a typical holiday.

**Pelajaran:** naik band bukan karena **kata sulit**, melainkan karena **detail spesifik**, **variasi struktur**, dan **kontrol waktu** (satu pembuka, satu cerita, satu alasan).

## 8. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

:::kesalahan-umum
Tiga kesalahan Part 2 yang paling merugikan: **berhenti terlalu cepat** (di bawah 1 menit), **melewatkan poin "explain why"**, dan **menghafal naskah** yang tidak sesuai kartu. Ketiganya menurunkan Fluency dan Coherence sekaligus. Daftar lengkapnya ada di tabel berikut.
:::

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Berhenti di **30–40 detik** | Target 1,5–2 menit | Fluency dinilai dari durasi dan kelancaran |
| 2 | Menulis **kalimat penuh** di catatan | Kata kunci | Waktu terbatas |
| 3 | **Melewatkan** poin *explain why* | Selalu jawab alasan/pendapat | Poin keempat sering dilupakan |
| 4 | Menghafal naskah | Gunakan catatan, bicara spontan | Terdengar tidak natural |
| 5 | Mencampur **tense** (past ke present) | Konsisten pada waktu cerita | Akurasi grammar |
| 6 | Hanya **menyebutkan fakta** (*I went. I ate. I came home.*) | Tambah perasaan, detail, alasan | Kurang berkembang |
| 7 | Mengulang **kata yang sama** (*good, nice, beautiful*) | Variasikan: *stunning, delicious, welcoming* | Lexical Resource |
| 8 | **Berhenti total** saat kehabisan ide | Pakai teknik pengisi (indra, perasaan, perbandingan) | Menjaga fluency |
| 9 | Menjawab pertanyaan lanjutan dengan **satu kata** | Jawab 1–2 kalimat | Penguji masih menilai |
| 10 | Bicara **terlalu cepat** karena gugup | Napas, jeda singkat | Kejelasan |
| 11 | Mengabaikan **struktur** (acak-acakan) | Pembuka, detail, alasan, penutup | Coherence |
| 12 | Terlalu **banyak kata isian** (*umm, ahh*) | *Well, ...* / *Let me see...* | Fluency |

## 9. Strategi IELTS: Persiapan dan Hari Tes

### 9.1 Latihan sehari-hari

| Latihan | Cara | Tujuan |
|---|---|---|
| **Timer 1 menit + 2 menit** | Ambil satu cue card, catat 1 menit, bicara 2 menit, rekam | Terbiasa dengan waktu |
| **Dengarkan rekaman** | Catat jeda dan kata yang diulang | Fluency dan lexical |
| **Perluas satu cerita** | Satu pengalaman bisa dipakai untuk banyak topik (orang, tempat, kejadian) | Efisiensi persiapan |
| **Latih 5 topik andalan** | Siapkan ide umum (orang, tempat, benda, pengalaman, aktivitas) | Keluwesan |

### 9.2 Saat tes

- **Baca kartu dua kali**, tandai kata kunci (*describe, explain why*).
- **Catat dengan kata kunci**, tentukan urutan.
- **Mulai dengan kalimat pembuka** dari catatan, lalu ikuti urutan.
- **Lihat catatan sesekali**, bukan membaca terus-menerus.
- **Gunakan topik yang kamu kuasai**, selama relevan; kejujuran fakta tidak dinilai.
- **Jika penguji menghentikan**, berhenti dengan tenang dan jawab pertanyaan lanjutan.

## 10. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**
- Part 2: kartu, **1 menit** catatan, **1–2 menit** bicara, lalu 1–2 pertanyaan lanjutan.
- Catatan = **kata kunci** per poin.
- Struktur: pembuka → detail → alasan → penutup.
- Isi waktu dengan detail indra, perasaan, perbandingan, dampak.
- Jangan berhenti sebelum 1,5 menit; jangan lewatkan poin *explain why*.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**
1. *Berapa lama waktu persiapan dan berapa lama bicara?*
2. *Apakah catatan sebaiknya kalimat lengkap atau kata kunci?*
3. *Poin kartu mana yang sering dilewatkan kandidat?*
4. *Sebutkan dua teknik mengisi waktu bila kehabisan ide.*
5. *Tense apa yang dominan untuk topik pengalaman masa lalu?*

**Kunci:** 1. 1 menit persiapan; 1–2 menit bicara. 2. Kata kunci. 3. *Explain why*. 4. Detail indra, perasaan, perbandingan, dampak, refleksi. 5. Past Simple (dan Past Continuous).
