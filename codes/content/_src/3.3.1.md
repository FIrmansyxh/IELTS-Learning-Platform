## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menulis laporan **Task 1 Academic** dengan struktur yang jelas: **introduction, overview, dan dua paragraf detail**.
2. Menguasai bahasa **tren** (naik, turun, stabil, puncak), **perbandingan**, dan **proporsi** untuk grafik garis, batang, lingkaran, dan tabel.
3. Mendeskripsikan **peta** (perubahan sebelum-sesudah) dan **diagram proses** dengan tense dan pasif yang tepat.
4. Memilih **fitur utama** dari data, bukan menyebut semua angka.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - Minimal **150 kata**, sekitar **20 menit**. Task 1 bernilai **sepertiga** skor Writing.
> - Struktur: **Introduction** (parafrase soal) → **Overview** (2 tren/ciri terbesar, tanpa angka detail) → **Detail 1** → **Detail 2**.
> - **Jangan beropini, jangan menjelaskan sebab** yang tidak ada di data. Lapor, bandingkan, rangkum.

> **Catatan platform:** sub-modul ini bertag **ACADEMIC**. Dalam mode General Training (bawaan), Task 1 berupa surat (lihat sub-modul 3.3.3), sehingga materi ini hanya tampil bila mode **ACADEMIC** diaktifkan.

## 1. Apa yang Dinilai di Task 1 Academic?

| Kriteria | Yang dilihat penguji | Contoh yang menurunkan skor |
|---|---|---|
| **Task Achievement** | Ada **overview**, fitur utama dipilih, data akurat | Menyebut semua angka tanpa pola; tanpa overview |
| **Coherence and Cohesion** | Paragraf teratur, penghubung tepat | Satu paragraf besar; "First, Second" berlebihan |
| **Lexical Resource** | Variasi kata tren dan perbandingan | Mengulang "increase" lima kali |
| **Grammatical Range and Accuracy** | Tense tepat, kalimat bervariasi | Tense campur; angka dan preposisi salah |

**Kesalahan paling mahal:** **tidak ada overview**. Tanpa overview, Task Achievement umumnya tertahan di band 5.

## 2. Struktur 4 Paragraf

:::flow
Introduction | Parafrase soal: jenis grafik, topik, periode
Overview | Dua fitur utama tanpa angka detail
Detail 1 | Fitur pertama dengan data
Detail 2 | Fitur kedua dengan data pembanding
:::

| Paragraf | Isi | Panjang | Contoh pembuka |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Introduction** | Parafrase soal (jenis grafik, topik, satuan, periode) | 1–2 kalimat | The line graph illustrates how... |
| **2. Overview** | 2–3 tren/fitur terbesar **tanpa angka detail** | 2–3 kalimat | Overall, ... **it is clear that**... |
| **3. Detail 1** | Fitur pertama dengan data pendukung | 3–4 kalimat | Looking at ..., |
| **4. Detail 2** | Fitur kedua dengan data pembanding | 3–4 kalimat | By contrast, ... |

### 2.1 Cara memarafrase soal (Introduction)

| Soal asli | Parafrase |
|---|---|
| The graph shows the number of ... | The line graph **illustrates** the **number of ...** |
| ... from 2000 to 2020 | **between** 2000 **and** 2020 / **over a 20-year period** |
| the percentage of | the **proportion** of / the **share** of |
| people in three countries | **residents** of three countries |

**Pola:** *The [chart type] **shows / illustrates / compares / outlines** [what] in [where] **between** [year] **and** [year].*

### 2.2 Cara menulis overview

| Jenis data | Overview yang baik |
|---|---|
| Tren waktu | **Overall**, A rose steadily, whereas B fluctuated and C declined. |
| Perbandingan | **Overall**, A was by far the largest category, while B was the smallest. |
| Proses | **Overall**, the process consists of five stages, beginning with... and ending with... |
| Peta | **Overall**, the village changed from a rural area into a more developed town. |

:::kesalahan-umum
**Overview bukan angka dan bukan kesimpulan pribadi.** Salah: *Overall, 45% of people used the internet in 2020.* (angka detail) / *Overall, the internet is very useful for people.* (opini). Benar: *Overall, internet use increased in every country, with Country A consistently the highest.*
:::

## 3. Bahasa Tren

### 3.1 Kata kerja dan kata benda

| Arah | Kata kerja | Kata benda | Contoh |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Naik** | rise, increase, grow, climb, go up | a rise, an increase, growth | Sales **rose** from 20 to 35. / There was **a rise** in sales. |
| **Naik tajam** | soar, jump, surge, rocket, shoot up | a jump, a surge | Prices **soared** to 90. |
| **Turun** | fall, decrease, decline, drop, go down | a fall, a decrease, a decline | Numbers **fell** to 12. |
| **Turun tajam** | plunge, plummet, collapse | a plunge, a sharp drop | Sales **plummeted**. |
| **Stabil** | remain stable/steady, stay constant, level off | stability, a plateau | It **remained steady** at 40. |
| **Berfluktuasi** | fluctuate, vary | fluctuations | Prices **fluctuated** between 30 and 50. |
| **Puncak / terendah** | peak at, reach a high of / bottom out, hit a low of | a peak, a low | It **peaked at** 80 in 2015. |

### 3.2 Adverbia dan adjektiva (kecepatan perubahan)

| Perubahan | Adverbia (untuk kata kerja) | Adjektiva (untuk kata benda) |
|---|---|---|
| Besar / cepat | dramatically, sharply, significantly, rapidly | dramatic, sharp, significant, rapid |
| Sedang | considerably, steadily, markedly | considerable, steady, marked |
| Kecil / lambat | slightly, gradually, marginally, modestly | slight, gradual, marginal, modest |

**Dua pola setara:**

- **Kata kerja + adverbia:** *Sales **rose sharply**.*
- **There was + adjektiva + kata benda:** ***There was a sharp rise** in sales.*

### 3.3 Preposisi (sumber kesalahan paling umum)

| Preposisi | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **from ... to ...** | titik awal dan akhir | rose **from** 20% **to** 35% |
| **by** | besarnya perubahan | rose **by** 15% |
| **at** | level tertentu / puncak | peaked **at** 80 |
| **to** | mencapai | rose **to** 90 |
| **between ... and ...** | rentang | fluctuated **between** 30 and 50 |
| **in / during / over** | periode | **in** 2010, **during** the 1990s, **over** the period |
| **of** | nilai | an increase **of** 10% |

:::tips-examiner
Variasikan kata, tetapi **pastikan makna tepat**: *soar* (naik sangat tajam) tidak cocok untuk kenaikan 2%. Satu kata tepat lebih baik daripada kata dramatis yang salah. Gunakan **kata benda** untuk variasi (*There was a gradual decline in...*) agar tidak terus-menerus memakai kata kerja.
:::

## 4. Bahasa Perbandingan dan Proporsi

### 4.1 Perbandingan

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **-er than / more ... than** | Sales in Asia were **higher than** in Europe. |
| **as ... as** | Spending on food was **as high as** spending on housing. |
| **the highest / the lowest** | Japan had **the highest** figure. |
| **twice / three times as ... as** | Rent was **twice as high as** food costs. |
| **whereas / while / by contrast** | A rose, **whereas** B fell. |
| **similar to / the same as / in line with** | Sweden followed a trend **similar to** Norway. |
| **the second highest / the third largest** | Chemicals were **the second largest** category. |

### 4.2 Proporsi dan angka

| Pecahan / persen | Cara menulis |
|---|---|
| 25% | a quarter, one in four |
| 50% | a half, half of |
| 75% | three quarters |
| 33% | a third, one third |
| 10% | one tenth, one in ten |
| sekitar 40% | **approximately / around / roughly / just under / just over** 40% |
| mayoritas | **the majority of**, **a large proportion of** |
| minoritas | **a small minority**, **a small proportion** |

## 5. Tense Menurut Rentang Waktu

| Situasi | Tense | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| Tahun-tahun di masa lampau (2000–2015) | **Simple Past** | The figure **rose** to 50% in 2010. |
| Periode sampai sekarang | **Present Perfect** | The figure **has risen** since 2010. |
| Masa depan (prediksi, 2030) | **will / is expected to / is projected to** | The population **is projected to reach** 9 billion. |
| Tidak ada waktu (tabel/pie chart statis) | **Simple Present** | Food **accounts for** 30% of spending. |
| Proses | **Simple Present passive** | The beans **are roasted**. |

:::kesalahan-umum
Jangan mencampur tense tanpa alasan. Jika grafik menunjukkan **2000–2015**, seluruh laporan memakai **Simple Past**. *Sales **rises** in 2005* adalah salah; *Sales **rose** in 2005* benar. Perhatikan juga preposisi: *rose **by** 10%* (besarnya) beda dengan *rose **to** 10%* (levelnya).
:::

## 6. Contoh Jawaban Model: Grafik Garis (Tren)

**Soal (contoh ilustrasi):** *The line graph shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries between 2000 and 2020.*

- **Band 5:** The graph shows the internet in three countries. Country A is high. Country B is low in 2000 and then it is high. Country C is increase. A is 90% in 2020.
- **Band 6:** The line graph shows the percentage of households with internet access in Country A, B, and C from 2000 to 2020. Country A increased from 40% to 90%. Country B increased from 10% to 75%, and Country C also increased.
- **Band 7:** The line graph compares the proportion of households with internet access in three countries over a 20-year period from 2000 to 2020.

  Overall, internet access rose in all three countries, with Country A consistently having the highest figures, whereas Country C remained the lowest throughout.

  In 2000, Country A led with 40% of households, followed by Country B at 10% and Country C at only 5%. Over the next decade, Country A grew steadily to reach 75% in 2010, while Country B rose sharply to 50%.

  By 2020, Country A peaked at 90%. Country B continued its rapid growth and reached 75%, narrowing the gap, whereas Country C rose more gradually to 35%.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) Ada **overview** yang jelas, (2) fitur dipilih (siapa tertinggi, siapa tercepat), bukan semua angka, (3) kosakata tren bervariasi dan akurat (*led, grew steadily, rose sharply, narrowing the gap*), (4) tense konsisten (Simple Past).

## 7. Diagram Proses

### 7.1 Ciri proses

| Jenis | Ciri | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear** | Awal dan akhir jelas | Pembuatan cokelat, kertas daur ulang |
| **Siklus** | Tidak ada awal/akhir | Siklus air, siklus hidup kupu-kupu |

### 7.2 Bahasa proses

| Fungsi | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| Pasif untuk tahap | **be + V3** | The beans **are harvested**. |
| Penanda urutan | First, Next, Then, After that, Subsequently, Following this, Finally | **Following this**, the paste is moulded. |
| Menghubungkan tahap | **Once / After + perfect**, V-ing | **Once the beans have been dried**, they are roasted. |
| Siklus | The cycle **begins with... and ends with... / then repeats** | The cycle **then repeats**. |
| Alat/bahan | **is used to + V1**, **is made from** | A press **is used to** extract the oil. |

### 7.3 Contoh paragraf (proses)

*The process begins when cocoa pods are harvested from the trees and split open to remove the beans. The beans are then fermented for a week, after which they are spread out and dried in the sun. Once dry, they are roasted at a high temperature and ground into a paste, which is finally moulded into bars and packaged.*

## 8. Peta (Perubahan Lokasi)

### 8.1 Bahasa peta

| Fungsi | Kata / pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Perubahan** | was replaced by, was converted into, was demolished, was constructed/built, was expanded, was relocated, disappeared | The farm **was converted into** a housing estate. |
| **Penambahan** | a new ... was added/introduced | A **new** car park **was added**. |
| **Lokasi** | to the north/south/east/west of, in the north-east, next to, opposite, between, adjacent to | The shop is **to the east of** the station. |
| **Ukuran** | enlarged, reduced, doubled in size | The park **was enlarged**. |
| **Tidak berubah** | remained unchanged, stayed the same | The school **remained unchanged**. |

### 8.2 Tense peta

| Situasi | Tense |
|---|---|
| Dua peta masa lalu (mis. 1990 dan 2010) | **Simple Past** (dan Past Perfect untuk yang lebih dulu) |
| Peta masa lalu dan **masa depan** (rencana) | Past + **will be / is planned to be** |
| Dua peta **sekarang** (sebelum/sesudah rencana) | Present |

### 8.3 Contoh paragraf (peta)

*Overall, the village changed from a quiet farming area into a busy residential and commercial centre. In 1990, farmland occupied most of the north, while a small school stood next to the river. By 2010, the farmland had been replaced by housing, and a supermarket had been built to the east of the station, opposite the new car park.*

## 9. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (tanpa overview) | Tambahkan paragraf overview | Wajib untuk Task Achievement. |
| 2 | The graph **shows about** the internet. | The graph **shows the percentage of** households with internet access. | Parafrase konkret. |
| 3 | Sales **rose to** 15% (selisih) | Sales **rose by** 15% | *By* = besarnya, *to* = level. |
| 4 | It **was increased** in 2005. | It **increased** in 2005. | *Increase* intransitive. |
| 5 | The number **raise** in 2010. | The number **rose** / **increased** | *Raise* transitive; *rise* intransitive. |
| 6 | The graph **explains why** prices fell. | The graph **shows that** prices fell. | Jangan menjelaskan sebab di luar data. |
| 7 | In my opinion, the trend is good. | (hapus) | Tidak ada opini di Task 1. |
| 8 | Prices **rises** in 2008. | Prices **rose** in 2008. | Tense lampau konsisten. |
| 9 | **The most people** | **The largest proportion of people** | Gunakan *proportion/percentage*. |
| 10 | 25% **people** | 25% **of people** | *Percent of + noun*. |
| 11 | Menulis **200+ kata** tentang setiap angka | Pilih fitur utama dalam **150–190** kata | Selektif lebih tinggi nilainya. |
| 12 | Tidak menyebut **satuan** | Cantumkan satuan (%, million, tonnes) | Data tidak lengkap tanpa satuan. |

## 10. Strategi IELTS: Rencana 20 Menit

| Menit | Kegiatan |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Baca soal, identifikasi **jenis grafik, satuan, periode**, lalu temukan **2 fitur utama** (tertinggi, terbesar, perubahan paling besar). |
| 3–5 | Rencanakan: Intro, Overview, Detail 1 (fitur A), Detail 2 (fitur B). |
| 5–17 | Tulis. Gunakan **Simple Past** (atau sesuai periode). |
| 17–20 | Periksa **angka, satuan, preposisi (by/to/at), tense**, dan jumlah kata (>= 150). |

**Aturan strategis:**

- **Kelompokkan data**: bandingkan *yang naik* vs *yang turun*, bukan menulis per tahun.
- **Selalu lampirkan data** pada klaim detail (*rose to 75% in 2010*).
- **Jangan menyalin** soal mentah-mentah sebagai Introduction.

## 11. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**

- Struktur: **Introduction → Overview → Detail 1 → Detail 2**; 150+ kata, 20 menit.
- Overview = tren/ciri terbesar tanpa angka detail. Tanpa overview = skor tertahan.
- Tren: rise/fall/fluctuate/peak; adverbia (sharply, steadily, slightly); preposisi *by/to/at/from...to*.
- Tense mengikuti periode data; proses = pasif + penanda urutan; peta = perubahan + lokasi.
- Tidak beropini, tidak menjelaskan sebab.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *Sales ___ (rise / raise) from 20 to 35 in 2010.*
2. *Prices rose ___ (by / to) 15% between 2000 and 2005.* (naik sebesar 15%)
3. *Which paragraph MUST contain no detailed numbers?* (Introduction / Overview / Detail 1)
4. *The farm ___ (converted) into a housing estate in 2005.* (pasif simple past)
5. *The figure peaked ___ (at / in) 80 in 2015.*

**Kunci:** 1. rose. 2. by. 3. Overview. 4. was converted. 5. at.
