## Mulai dari Sini

:::pengantar
**Apa yang kamu pelajari?** Bagian ini membahas kata penghubung, yaitu kata kecil yang menyambung dua gagasan, seperti "dan", "tetapi", "karena", dan "walaupun". Dalam bahasa Inggris ada dua kelompok besar. *Conjunction* (kata sambung) menyambung dua bagian di dalam satu kalimat, misalnya *I was tired, so I went to bed*. *Discourse marker* (penanda hubungan antarkalimat) menyambung satu kalimat dengan kalimat berikutnya, misalnya *However* di awal kalimat baru. Ada satu beda penting dengan bahasa Indonesia. Kita biasa berkata "walaupun hujan, tetapi kami tetap pergi", sedangkan bahasa Inggris hanya mengizinkan satu penghubung untuk satu hubungan: *Although it rained, we went out*, tanpa *but*.

**Kenapa ini penting?** Tanpa penghubung, tulisanmu terasa seperti deretan kalimat pendek yang berdiri sendiri-sendiri, dan pembaca harus menebak hubungan antargagasannya. Di IELTS, cara kamu menyambung gagasan ikut dinilai di Writing lewat kriteria *Coherence and Cohesion* (tulisan runtut dan tersambung rapi). Penghubung juga menjadi petunjuk arah saat kamu mengerjakan Listening dan Reading.

**Bekal sebelum mulai:** Kamu sebaiknya sudah bisa membuat kalimat sederhana dengan subjek dan kata kerja, seperti yang dilatih di 1.1.2 (Present Simple vs. Present Continuous) dan 1.1.3 (Past Simple vs. Present Perfect). Halaman ini memuat banyak daftar kata, dan kamu tidak perlu menghafalnya sekaligus. Pahami dulu tiga jenis penghubung di Bagian 1, lalu pakai tabel lainnya sebagai rujukan saat menulis.
:::

### Istilah yang Akan Kamu Temui

| Istilah | Arti sederhana | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Clause (klausa)** | Kelompok kata yang punya subjek dan kata kerja sendiri | *it was cold* |
| **Conjunction** | Kata sambung di dalam satu kalimat | *and, but, because* |
| **Coordinating conjunction** | Kata sambung untuk dua klausa yang setara | It was cold, **but** we went out. |
| **Subordinating conjunction** | Kata sambung yang membuat satu klausa bergantung pada klausa lain | **Although** it was cold, we went out. |
| **Discourse marker** | Kata atau frasa penghubung antarkalimat | *However, Therefore, In addition* |
| **Comma splice** | Kesalahan: dua kalimat lengkap hanya dipisah koma | *It was raining, we stayed home.* (salah) |
| **Coherence and Cohesion** | Kriteria nilai Writing: tulisan runtut dan tersambung rapi | Gagasan disambung dengan *However* atau *For example* |

## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Menghubungkan ide dengan **coordinating conjunction**, **subordinating conjunction**, dan **discourse marker** secara tepat, termasuk tanda bacanya.
2. Membedakan **although / despite / however / but** dan **because / because of / therefore / so**.
3. Menghindari **comma splice**, kalimat ganda ("Although ... but"), dan kesalahan pola *despite of*.
4. Memakai penghubung yang bervariasi agar skor **Coherence and Cohesion** naik.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - **Conjunction** menghubungkan klausa *dalam satu kalimat* (and, but, because, although).
> - **Discourse marker** menghubungkan ide *antar kalimat* (However, Therefore, In addition).
> - Satu hubungan logis = **satu** penghubung. *Although it rained, we went out* (benar). *Although it rained, but we went out* (salah).

## 1. Tiga Jenis Penghubung

Semua kata penghubung bahasa Inggris masuk ke salah satu dari tiga jenis. Bedanya ada pada apa yang disambung dan tanda baca yang menyertainya. Di tabel kamu akan sering bertemu kata klausa, yaitu kelompok kata yang punya subjek dan kata kerja sendiri, misalnya *it was cold*. Baca kotak perbandingan dulu, lalu lihat contohnya di tabel.

:::compare
Coordinating (FANBOYS) | Menyetarakan dua klausa; koma sebelum konjungsi; but, so, and, or
Subordinating | Satu klausa bergantung pada klausa lain; although, because, while, unless
Discourse marker | Menghubungkan antar kalimat; However, Therefore, Moreover; diikuti koma
:::

| Jenis | Fungsi | Posisi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coordinating conjunction** | Menyetarakan dua klausa | Di tengah, setelah koma | It was cold, **but** we went out. |
| **Subordinating conjunction** | Membuat satu klausa bergantung pada klausa lain | Di awal atau tengah kalimat | **Although** it was cold, we went out. |
| **Conjunctive adverb / discourse marker** | Menghubungkan dua kalimat atau klausa dengan makna logis | Awal kalimat atau setelah titik koma | It was cold. **However**, we went out. |

## 2. Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

Jenis pertama adalah tujuh kata sambung pendek yang menyambung dua klausa setara, artinya keduanya sama penting dan masing-masing bisa berdiri sendiri sebagai kalimat. Supaya mudah diingat, huruf awal ketujuh kata itu disingkat menjadi FANBOYS. Untuk tahap ini, pastikan dulu kamu lancar memakai *and*, *but*, *or*, dan *so*.

| Kata | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **F**or | Alasan (formal) | She stayed home, **for** she was ill. |
| **A**nd | Tambahan | I studied, **and** I took notes. |
| **N**or | Tidak juga | He didn't call, **nor** did he write. |
| **B**ut | Kontras | It was cheap, **but** it broke quickly. |
| **O**r | Pilihan | We can walk, **or** we can take a bus. |
| **Y**et | Kontras mengejutkan | He is young, **yet** he is very wise. |
| **S**o | Akibat | I was tired, **so** I went to bed. |

**Aturan tanda baca:** antara dua klausa lengkap, taruh **koma** sebelum konjungsi. Jika hanya satu subjek dengan dua kata kerja, tanpa koma: *She opened the door and walked in.*

:::kesalahan-umum
**Comma splice** = dua klausa lengkap hanya dipisah koma. Salah: *It was raining, we stayed home.* Benar: *It was raining, **so** we stayed home.* / *It was raining; we stayed home.* / *It was raining. We stayed home.*
:::

#### Correlative conjunctions (kata sambung berpasangan)

Selain FANBOYS, ada kata sambung setara yang selalu datang berpasangan. Dua bagian yang disambung harus sejenis (noun dengan noun, verb dengan verb). Yang paling sering salah adalah bentuk kata kerjanya, jadi perhatikan kolom terakhir.

| Pasangan | Arti | Kata kerja mengikuti | Contoh |
|---|---|---|---|
| **both** A **and** B | A dan B, dua-duanya | Selalu jamak | **Both** Mira **and** Joni **go** to Surabaya. |
| **not only** A **but also** B | tidak hanya A, tetapi juga B | Subjek terdekat (B) | **Not only** my parents **but also** my sister **calls** me every week. |
| **either** A **or** B | A atau B, salah satu | Subjek terdekat (B) | **Either** the manager **or** the assistants **are** in the office. |
| **neither** A **nor** B | bukan A dan bukan B | Subjek terdekat (B) | **Neither** the students **nor** the teacher **knows** the answer. |

Dua hal perlu diingat. Pertama, *neither ... nor* sudah bermakna negatif, jadi jangan ditambah *not*: *I like neither tea nor coffee*, bukan *I don't like neither tea nor coffee*. Kedua, bila *not only* diletakkan di awal kalimat untuk menyambung dua klausa, susunan klausa pertama dibalik seperti kalimat tanya: *Not only **does he speak** English, but he also speaks Japanese.* Susunan terbalik ini (inversion) dibahas lebih lanjut di Tier 4.

## 3. Subordinating Conjunctions (menurut fungsi)

Jenis kedua membuat satu klausa bergantung pada klausa lain. Contohnya *because I was sick*: potongan ini belum lengkap dan baru bermakna setelah ditempelkan ke klausa utama, *I stayed home*. Tabel di bawah mengelompokkan kata sambung ini menurut maknanya (kontras, sebab, waktu, syarat, tujuan, akibat), jadi cari dulu makna yang kamu butuhkan.

### 3.1 Daftar lengkap

Konsesi di baris pertama berarti mengakui satu hal dulu, lalu menyatakan hal yang berlawanan dengannya ("memang..., tetapi tetap...").

| Fungsi | Penghubung | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Kontras / konsesi** | although, though, even though, while, whereas | **Although** he studied hard, he failed. |
| **Sebab** | because, since, as | I stayed home **because** I was sick. |
| **Waktu** | when, while, before, after, until, as soon as, once | Call me **as soon as** you arrive. |
| **Syarat** | if, unless, as long as, provided that | **Unless** you hurry, you'll be late. |
| **Tujuan** | so that, in order that | I saved money **so that** I could travel. |
| **Akibat** | so ... that, such ... that | It was **so** hot **that** we stayed inside. |

### 3.2 Posisi klausa dan koma

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Konjungsi + klausa, klausa utama** (pakai koma) | **Although** it was late, she kept working. |
| **Klausa utama + konjungsi + klausa** (biasanya tanpa koma) | She kept working **although** it was late. |

### 3.3 Variasi contoh

#### Level 1: dasar

1. I like tea, **but** my brother prefers coffee.
2. **Because** it was raining, we stayed inside.
3. Wait here **until** I come back.

#### Level 2: sehari-hari (Speaking)

1. **Although** I'm not a great cook, I really enjoy trying new recipes.
2. I usually study in the library **because** it's quiet, **whereas** my brother prefers working at home.
3. **As soon as** I got home, I realised I had left my keys at the office.

#### Level 3: akademik (Writing Task 2)

1. **While** some people believe that technology isolates us, others argue that it brings communities closer.
2. **Unless** governments take immediate action, carbon emissions will continue to rise.
3. Public transport should be improved **so that** fewer people depend on private cars.

## 4. Discourse Markers (Penghubung Antar Kalimat)

Jenis ketiga bekerja di antara kalimat, bukan di dalam kalimat. Kata seperti *However* atau *Therefore* biasanya berdiri di awal kalimat baru, diikuti koma, dan memberi tahu pembaca hubungan kalimat itu dengan kalimat sebelumnya: menambah, melawan, atau menyimpulkan. Tabelnya panjang, jadi cukup kuasai satu atau dua kata per fungsi dulu.

Ini adalah alat utama untuk **Coherence and Cohesion**. Pilih berdasarkan fungsi.

| Fungsi | Discourse marker | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Tambahan** | In addition, Moreover, Furthermore, Besides, Also, What is more | Public transport is cheap. **Moreover**, it reduces traffic. |
| **Kontras** | However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, In contrast, By contrast | The plan is cheap. **However**, it may take too long. |
| **Konsesi** | Even so, Still, All the same | The film was long. **Even so**, I enjoyed it. |
| **Sebab-akibat** | Therefore, Consequently, As a result, Thus, Hence, For this reason | Prices rose. **As a result**, fewer people bought cars. |
| **Contoh** | For example, For instance, In particular | Many cities face congestion. **For example**, Jakarta... |
| **Urutan** | First, Second, Then, After that, Finally, Subsequently | **First**, boil the water. **Then**, add the rice. |
| **Penegasan** | In fact, Indeed, Clearly, Obviously | **In fact**, the results were better than expected. |
| **Ringkasan / kesimpulan** | In conclusion, To sum up, Overall, In short | **Overall**, the benefits outweigh the costs. |

#### Aturan tanda baca discourse marker

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Titik + Marker + koma** | It rained all day. **However,** we enjoyed the trip. |
| **Titik koma + marker + koma** | It rained all day; **however,** we enjoyed the trip. |
| **Di tengah (setelah subjek), diapit koma** | We, **however,** enjoyed the trip. |

:::tips-examiner
Jangan memakai penghubung **terlalu banyak atau mekanis** (*Firstly... Secondly... Thirdly...* di setiap paragraf). Penguji Band 7 menyukai penghubung yang **bervariasi dan alami**, serta cohesion yang dibangun lewat **pronoun** (*this, these, such*) dan **referensi** (*This trend...*, *The latter...*). Satu penghubung yang tepat lebih baik daripada tiga yang dipaksakan.
:::

## 5. Pasangan yang Sering Tertukar

Beberapa penghubung punya arti yang sama tetapi aturan pakainya berbeda. *Although* dan *despite* sama-sama berarti "walaupun", tetapi kata yang boleh mengikutinya tidak sama. Kuncinya ada di kolom "Diikuti": apakah sesudahnya klausa (ditulis S + V, subjek dan kata kerja), atau cukup kata benda (*noun*) atau kata kerja berakhiran *-ing* (V-ing).

### 5.1 Kontras: although / despite / however / but

| Penghubung | Diikuti | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|---|
| **although / though / even though** | klausa (S + V) | Although + S + V, S + V | **Although** it was raining, we played football. |
| **despite / in spite of** | **noun / V-ing** | Despite + noun/V-ing, S + V | **Despite** the rain, we played football. **Despite** being tired, she finished. |
| **despite the fact that** | klausa | | **Despite the fact that** it was raining, we played. |
| **but** | klausa (tengah) | S + V, but S + V | It was raining, **but** we played football. |
| **however** | kalimat baru | S + V. However, S + V | It was raining. **However**, we played football. |
| **whereas / while** | membandingkan dua hal | S + V, whereas S + V | Rich countries use more energy, **whereas** poor countries use less. |

### 5.2 Sebab dan akibat: because / because of / therefore / so

| Penghubung | Diikuti | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **because / since / as** | klausa | I stayed home **because** I was ill. |
| **because of / due to / owing to** | noun/frasa | I stayed home **because of** illness. |
| **so / therefore / consequently** | akibat | I was ill, **so** I stayed home. / I was ill. **Therefore**, I stayed home. |

### 5.3 Tabel pola cepat

| Makna | Dua klausa | Dengan noun |
|---|---|---|
| Kontras | although + S V | despite / in spite of + noun/V-ing |
| Sebab | because + S V | because of / due to + noun |
| Akibat | so / therefore + S V | as a result of + noun |

## 6. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

Banyak kesalahan di tabel ini lahir dari kebiasaan bahasa Indonesia, terutama memasang dua penghubung sekaligus seperti "walaupun... tetapi" dan "karena... maka". Tutup kolom "Perbaikan", coba betulkan sendiri kalimatnya, lalu cocokkan dengan jawabannya.

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Although** it rained, **but** we went out. | **Although** it rained, we went out. | Dalam bahasa Indonesia "walaupun... tetapi" boleh dipasangkan. Dalam bahasa Inggris, satu penghubung saja. |
| 2 | **Despite of** the rain... | **Despite** the rain... / **In spite of** the rain... | *Despite* tidak memakai *of*. |
| 3 | **Because** it rained, **so** we stayed home. | **Because** it rained, we stayed home. | Sama seperti nomor 1: *because* dan *so* tidak bersamaan. |
| 4 | **Because of** it was late... | **Because** it was late... / **Because of** the late hour... | *Because of* diikuti noun, bukan klausa. |
| 5 | **Despite** he was tired... | **Although** he was tired... / **Despite** being tired... | *Despite* diikuti noun atau V-ing. |
| 6 | It was cold, we went out. | It was cold, **but** we went out. | Comma splice. |
| 7 | **However** I like it. (tanpa koma) | **However,** I like it. | Marker di awal kalimat diikuti koma. |
| 8 | He is rich, **but also** he is kind. | He is rich **and** kind. | *But also* hanya dalam pola *not only... but also*. |
| 9 | **Firstly**, ..., **Secondly**, ..., di setiap paragraf | Variasikan: First of all, In addition, Another point is | Mekanis dan monoton. |
| 10 | **In the other hand** | **On the other hand** | Frasa tetap. |
| 11 | **Moreover, however...** | Pilih satu | Dua makna berlawanan di satu kalimat. |
| 12 | **At last** (untuk menutup esai) | **In conclusion** | *At last* = akhirnya, setelah penantian. |

## 7. Strategi IELTS

Bagian ini menunjukkan penghubung mana yang cocok untuk tiap bagian tes, karena esai, surat, dan percakapan menuntut gaya yang berbeda. Kamu tidak perlu membaca semuanya sekaligus. Mulai dari butir Writing kalau sedang berlatih menulis, atau butir Listening dan Reading kalau sedang berlatih memahami.

- **Writing Task 2:** setiap paragraf badan sebaiknya punya **minimal satu kontras** (*However, Whereas*), **satu penambahan** (*In addition, Moreover*), dan **satu contoh** (*For example*). Hindari menumpuk marker di setiap kalimat.
- **Writing Task 1 Academic:** kontras data (*whereas, while, in contrast*), urutan proses (*First, Then, After that, Finally*), dan sebab (*due to, as a result of*).
- **Writing Task 1 GT (surat):** *I am writing to complain...*, *Furthermore, ...*, *I would therefore be grateful if...*. Hindari penghubung terlalu informal di surat formal (*Besides, Plus*).
- **Speaking:** penghubung alami untuk fluency: *Well, because..., Actually, ... although..., On the whole, ... the thing is..., so...* Hindari menghafal blok marker akademik berlebihan.
- **Listening:** perhatikan **however, but, although, actually, in fact**. Informasi yang benar sering berada **setelah** penanda kontras (*I first thought... but in fact...*).
- **Reading:** penghubung menunjukkan hubungan antar ide dan membantu Yes/No/Not Given. *Although the author admits..., he argues...* berarti opini penulis ada pada klausa kedua.

## 8. Contoh Jawaban Model (Writing Task 2, satu paragraf)

Satu soal esai dijawab dalam tiga tingkat kemampuan. Gagasannya mirip, tetapi cara menyambungnya berbeda jauh. Saat membaca, tandai setiap penghubung di tiap versi dan perhatikan: versi Band 5 memasang *Because... so* bersamaan, sedangkan versi Band 7 memakai penghubung yang beragam dengan tanda baca yang benar.

**Soal:** *Some people think children should start learning a foreign language at primary school. Discuss.*

- **Band 5:** Children learn language at primary school is good. Because brain is young, so they learn fast. But some people think no good.
- **Band 6:** Learning a foreign language in primary school is beneficial because children learn quickly. However, some people think it is too difficult, and they argue that children should focus on their own language first.
- **Band 7:** Introducing a foreign language in primary school is widely considered beneficial, as young children absorb new sounds and vocabulary more easily than adults. Nevertheless, critics argue that early exposure may interfere with children's mastery of their mother tongue. Although this concern is understandable, research suggests that bilingual children often perform better academically; consequently, schools should adopt a balanced approach.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) Penghubung **bervariasi dan tepat** (*as, Nevertheless, Although, consequently*), bukan hanya *because* dan *but*, (2) tidak ada penghubung ganda (*Because... so...*), (3) tanda baca benar (titik koma sebelum *consequently*).

## 9. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

Empat butir "Ingat" di bawah adalah inti halaman ini. Sesudah membacanya, kerjakan lima soal Cek Diri tanpa menggulir ke atas. Kalau salah di soal tentang *despite* atau *because of*, baca lagi Bagian 5 sebelum mulai drill.

**Ingat:**

- Satu hubungan logis = satu penghubung.
- although + klausa; despite + noun/V-ing; because + klausa; because of + noun.
- However / Therefore / Moreover = awal kalimat + koma (atau setelah titik koma).
- Hindari comma splice; hindari penghubung mekanis.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *___ (Although / Despite) the traffic, we arrived on time.*
2. *He missed the bus ___ (because / because of) his alarm didn't ring.*
3. *The film was long. ___ (However, / But) I enjoyed it.*
4. *Prices rose sharply; ___ (therefore / although), fewer people bought cars.*
5. *She was tired, ___ (so / because) she went to bed early.*

**Kunci:** 1. Despite. 2. because. 3. However,. 4. therefore. 5. so.
