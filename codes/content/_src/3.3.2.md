## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Mengenali **empat jenis soal Task 2** (opinion, discussion, problem-solution, two-part) dan memilih kerangka yang tepat.
2. Menulis esai **4 paragraf** dengan **thesis** yang jelas dan paragraf badan yang berkembang (**PEEL**).
3. Merencanakan dan menulis **250+ kata dalam 40 menit**.
4. Menghindari kesalahan utama: **tidak menjawab semua bagian soal**, ide tidak berkembang, dan hafalan template.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - **250 kata minimum, 40 menit.** Task 2 bernilai **dua pertiga** skor Writing.
> - Kerangka: **Introduction → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion.**
> - Skor terbesar ada di **Task Response**: jawab **semua bagian** soal dengan **posisi yang jelas** dan **ide yang dikembangkan**.

---

## 1. Apa yang Dinilai?

| Kriteria | Yang dilihat penguji |
|---|---|
| **Task Response** | Semua bagian soal dijawab, posisi jelas di seluruh esai, ide dikembangkan dengan penjelasan dan contoh |
| **Coherence and Cohesion** | Satu ide utama per paragraf, urutan logis, penghubung alami |
| **Lexical Resource** | Kosakata tepat dan bervariasi, kolokasi, sedikit kesalahan ejaan |
| **Grammatical Range and Accuracy** | Kalimat sederhana dan kompleks, kesalahan tidak mengganggu makna |

Setiap kriteria bernilai **25%**.

## 2. Empat Jenis Soal dan Cara Menjawabnya

### 2.1 Ringkasan

:::compare
Opinion | Nyatakan setuju atau tidak; satu posisi konsisten
Discussion | Bahas kedua pandangan, lalu beri opini
Problem-Solution | Jelaskan penyebab dan solusi
Two-Part | Jawab kedua pertanyaan, satu paragraf masing-masing
:::

| Jenis | Ciri soal | Yang harus dilakukan | Posisi |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opinion (Agree/Disagree)** | *To what extent do you agree or disagree?* | Nyatakan setuju, tidak setuju, atau sebagian, lalu pertahankan | **Satu posisi** konsisten |
| **Discussion (Discuss both views)** | *Discuss both views and give your opinion.* | Bahas **kedua** pandangan, lalu berikan opini | Opini di intro dan kesimpulan |
| **Problem-Solution** | *What are the causes? What can be done?* | Jelaskan **masalah/penyebab** dan **solusi** | Rekomendasi jelas |
| **Two-Part Question** | Dua pertanyaan terpisah | Jawab **keduanya**, satu paragraf per pertanyaan | Jawaban jelas tiap bagian |

:::kesalahan-umum
**Jawaban sebagian** adalah kesalahan paling mahal. Soal *Discuss both views and give your opinion* memuat **tiga tugas** (pandangan 1, pandangan 2, opinimu). Soal dua pertanyaan memuat dua jawaban. Garis bawahi setiap tugas sebelum menulis dan centang saat kamu sudah menjawabnya. Esai yang hanya menjawab satu bagian tertahan di sekitar **band 5**.
:::

### 2.2 Kerangka tiap jenis

| Jenis | Intro | Body 1 | Body 2 | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Opinion** | Parafrase + **thesis** (posisi) | Alasan utama 1 | Alasan utama 2 | Ulangi posisi |
| **Discussion** | Parafrase + **outline** kedua pandangan + opini | Pandangan A | Pandangan B (+ opini) | Rangkum + opini |
| **Problem-Solution** | Parafrase + 2 masalah dan solusi | Masalah/penyebab | Solusi | Rangkum |
| **Two-Part** | Parafrase + dua jawaban singkat | Jawaban pertanyaan 1 | Jawaban pertanyaan 2 | Rangkum |

## 3. Anatomi Esai 4 Paragraf

:::flow
Introduction | Parafrase soal dan thesis
Body 1 | Satu ide utama dengan PEEL
Body 2 | Ide utama kedua dengan PEEL
Conclusion | Ulangi posisi dan rangkum
:::

### 3.1 Introduction (2–3 kalimat, ± 40 kata)

| Kalimat | Fungsi | Pola |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parafrase** topik (bukan menyalin soal) | *It is often argued that... / Some people believe that...* |
| 2 | **Thesis / outline** (posisi atau isi esai) | *This essay will argue that... / This essay will discuss both views before... / This essay will examine the causes and propose solutions.* |

**Cara memarafrase:** ganti kata kunci dengan sinonim dan ubah struktur kalimat.

| Soal | Parafrase |
|---|---|
| *Many people think that children spend too much time on screens.* | *There is a widespread belief that young people spend excessive time in front of electronic devices.* |

### 3.2 Body paragraph (± 90–100 kata): kerangka PEEL

| Huruf | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **P**oint | Kalimat topik: ide utama paragraf | **Firstly, excessive screen time can harm children's health.** |
| **E**xplanation | Menjelaskan *mengapa/bagaimana* | **Spending hours sitting down reduces physical activity and disrupts sleep.** |
| **E**xample | Contoh konkret (bisa umum, tak perlu data asli) | **For instance, children who use tablets late at night often struggle to concentrate at school.** |
| **L**ink | Menghubungkan kembali ke soal/thesis | **Therefore, limiting screen time is essential for healthy development.** |

:::tips-examiner
Satu paragraf badan = **satu ide utama** yang **dikembangkan**. Jangan memasukkan tiga ide pendek dalam satu paragraf. Penguji Band 7 mencari ide yang **dijelaskan dan dicontohkan**, bukan daftar poin. Contoh boleh berupa situasi umum atau hipotetis (*For example, a student who...*); kamu tidak perlu mengutip statistik.
:::

### 3.3 Conclusion (2 kalimat, ± 30 kata)

- Gunakan **In conclusion / To sum up / Overall**.
- **Ulangi posisi** dengan kata berbeda dan rangkum alasan utama.
- **Jangan** memperkenalkan ide baru.

*In conclusion, although technology brings undeniable benefits, I firmly believe that parents should limit screen time to protect children's health.*

## 4. Kerangka Lengkap per Jenis Soal (dengan contoh)

### 4.1 Opinion

**Soal:** *Some people think university education should be free for all students. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

| Paragraf | Isi |
|---|---|
| Intro | Parafrase + **posisi**: *I largely agree...* |
| Body 1 | Alasan 1: akses setara (*students from low-income families...*) |
| Body 2 | Alasan 2: manfaat ekonomi bagi negara (*a more skilled workforce...*) |
| Conclusion | Ulangi posisi |

### 4.2 Discussion

**Soal:** *Some believe homework should be banned, while others think it is essential. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*

| Paragraf | Isi |
|---|---|
| Intro | Parafrase + kedua pandangan + opini singkat |
| Body 1 | Mengapa PR dianggap perlu (disiplin, latihan) |
| Body 2 | Mengapa PR dilarang (stres, kurang waktu keluarga) + **opini** |
| Conclusion | Rangkum + opini |

### 4.3 Problem-Solution

**Soal:** *In many cities, traffic congestion is a serious problem. What are the causes, and what can be done?*

| Paragraf | Isi |
|---|---|
| Intro | Parafrase + dua bagian soal |
| Body 1 | **Penyebab**: banyak kendaraan pribadi, transportasi umum buruk |
| Body 2 | **Solusi**: investasi transportasi umum, pajak kendaraan, jalur sepeda |
| Conclusion | Rangkum |

### 4.4 Two-Part Question

**Soal:** *More people are choosing to work from home. Why is this? Is it a positive or negative development?*

| Paragraf | Isi |
|---|---|
| Intro | Parafrase + jawaban singkat keduanya |
| Body 1 | Pertanyaan 1: **alasan** (teknologi, hemat waktu) |
| Body 2 | Pertanyaan 2: **positif atau negatif** (nilai dan alasan) |
| Conclusion | Rangkum |

## 5. Contoh Jawaban Model: Esai Lengkap (Opinion, ± 290 kata)

**Soal:** *Some people think that children should start learning a foreign language at primary school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

**Introduction**
It is often argued that foreign language education should begin in primary school rather than later. I strongly agree with this view because young children learn languages more easily, and early learning brings long-term academic and social benefits.

**Body 1**
Firstly, children are naturally more receptive to new sounds and vocabulary than adults. Their brains are still developing, which allows them to imitate pronunciation accurately and absorb grammar without conscious effort. For example, a child who attends an English-medium class from the age of six will usually speak with more natural intonation than a teenager who starts later. Therefore, starting early gives learners a significant advantage that is difficult to replicate in adulthood.

**Body 2**
Secondly, learning a second language improves overall academic performance. Bilingual children often show stronger memory and problem-solving skills, which helps them in subjects such as mathematics and science. In addition, speaking another language increases cultural awareness, so students become more open-minded and better prepared for a globalised workplace. Although some critics claim that an early start may confuse children, research generally suggests that children adapt quickly, provided that lessons are well designed.

**Conclusion**
In conclusion, I believe that introducing foreign languages in primary school is beneficial for linguistic, academic, and cultural reasons. Governments should therefore make early language education a priority.

**Mengapa Band 7+?** (1) Posisi **konsisten dan jelas** sejak intro sampai conclusion, (2) setiap body = satu ide utama yang **dijelaskan dan dicontohkan** (PEEL), (3) penghubung bervariasi (*Firstly, Therefore, In addition, Although*), (4) menyebut **sisi lawan** secara singkat tanpa kehilangan posisi, (5) kalimat kompleks yang akurat.

## 6. Perbandingan Level Band (paragraf badan)

**Topik:** *Is it good for children to start school at a very young age?*

- **Band 5:** School is good for children because they learn many thing. Children can study math and reading. Also it is good for parents because they work. So I think start school young is good.
- **Band 6:** Starting school at a young age has several advantages. Firstly, children learn basic skills such as reading and counting. Secondly, parents can work while their children are at school. Therefore, early schooling is beneficial.
- **Band 7:** One compelling reason for starting school early is that it lays the foundation for lifelong learning. During the early years, children develop literacy and numeracy skills that form the basis of all later subjects. For instance, a child who learns to read confidently at the age of five is far more likely to enjoy books and perform well throughout primary school. As a result, early schooling can significantly improve long-term academic outcomes.

**Pelajaran:** naik band bukan karena kata sulit, melainkan karena **ide dikembangkan** (penjelasan + contoh + akibat) dan **kontrol tata bahasa**.

## 7. Bahasa Esai: Frasa dan Penghubung

| Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **Menyatakan opini** | I firmly believe that..., In my opinion..., I largely agree that..., It is my view that... |
| **Memperkenalkan alasan** | One key reason is that..., A further advantage is..., Another significant factor is... |
| **Menambahkan** | In addition, Moreover, Furthermore, What is more, |
| **Kontras** | However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, Whereas |
| **Contoh** | For example, For instance, A case in point is..., Take ... as an example |
| **Sebab-akibat** | As a result, Consequently, This leads to..., Therefore, |
| **Konsesi** | Although..., While it is true that..., Admittedly, |
| **Kesimpulan** | In conclusion, To sum up, Overall, |

**Hedging (bahasa hati-hati):** *tend to, may, can, often, in many cases, it is likely that.* Hindari kata mutlak (*always, never, everyone, all, nobody*) yang mudah dibantah.

## 8. Rencana 40 Menit

| Menit | Kegiatan |
|---|---|
| **0–5** | Analisis soal: jenis, **tugas yang harus dijawab**, kata kunci. Tentukan posisi. |
| **5–8** | Rencana: 2 ide utama + penjelasan/contoh singkat untuk tiap body. |
| **8–35** | Tulis: Intro (3–4 menit), Body 1 (9), Body 2 (9), Conclusion (3). |
| **35–40** | Periksa: **semua bagian soal terjawab?** jumlah kata, -s/-ed, artikel, ejaan, tanda baca. |

**Aturan strategis:**

- **Jangan menyalin soal** mentah-mentah pada introduction.
- **Jangan menghafal esai** atau template utuh: penguji mengenali jawaban hafalan dan mengurangi skor.
- **Jangan terlalu pendek** (< 250 kata mengurangi Task Response) atau terlalu panjang (> 320 kata meningkatkan risiko kesalahan).

## 9. Mengembangkan Ide dengan Cepat

**Alat bantu: pertanyaan "mengapa?" dan "lalu apa?"**

| Ide awal | Mengapa? | Lalu apa? |
|---|---|---|
| Public transport reduces traffic. | Fewer people use private cars. | Air pollution and travel time decrease. |
| Homework teaches responsibility. | Students must manage their own time. | They develop discipline needed for university. |

**Sumber ide yang selalu tersedia:** *kesehatan, ekonomi, pendidikan, lingkungan, keluarga, teknologi, budaya, keamanan.* Pilih dua sudut pandang yang berbeda untuk dua paragraf badan.

## 10. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hanya menjawab **satu** bagian soal | Jawab semua bagian | Task Response tertahan di band 5. |
| 2 | Tidak ada posisi yang jelas | Nyatakan posisi di intro dan conclusion | Posisi harus konsisten. |
| 3 | Salin soal di intro | Parafrase | Salinan tidak dihitung. |
| 4 | Daftar ide pendek dalam satu paragraf | Satu ide utama per paragraf, dikembangkan | Kedalaman > jumlah ide. |
| 5 | **I think... In my opinion... I believe...** berulang | Variasikan frasa | Monoton. |
| 6 | Kata mutlak: *Everyone knows that...* | *Many people believe that...* | Hedging lebih akurat. |
| 7 | **Firstly, secondly, thirdly** mekanis di tiap paragraf | Penghubung bervariasi | Terlihat dihafal. |
| 8 | Esai hafalan dengan topik tidak cocok | Jawab soal yang diberikan | Off-topic dikenai penalti. |
| 9 | **People** + kata kerja tunggal (*people is*) | *People are* | Subjek-kata kerja. |
| 10 | Tidak ada contoh | Tambahkan *For example, ...* | Ide tanpa contoh kurang meyakinkan. |
| 11 | Ide baru di conclusion | Hanya rangkum | Penguji menilai kohesi. |
| 12 | Kalimat sangat panjang tanpa titik | Pecah jadi 2 kalimat | Akurasi lebih penting daripada panjang. |

## 11. Strategi IELTS

- **Analisis soal 5 menit pertama** adalah investasi terbaik. Tulis **tugas** (mis. "view A", "view B", "my opinion") dan centang saat sudah dijawab.
- **Satu esai = satu argumen**: pastikan tiap paragraf badan mendukung thesis.
- **Gunakan kalimat kompleks yang aman**: relative clause (*which*), conditional (*If governments invested..., ... would...*), pasif (*It is widely believed that...*). Lihat Modul 2.1.
- **Akurasi** lebih penting daripada kerumitan: kalimat sederhana yang benar lebih baik daripada kalimat panjang yang salah.
- **Konsisten dengan register formal**: tanpa singkatan (*don't, can't*), tanpa slang, tanpa *you/I* berlebihan.
- **Waktu**: sisakan 5 menit akhir untuk mengecek artikel, bentuk jamak, subjek-kata kerja, dan koma.

## 12. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**

- 4 jenis soal: opinion, discussion, problem-solution, two-part. **Jawab semua bagian.**
- Kerangka: Intro (parafrase + thesis) → Body 1 → Body 2 (PEEL) → Conclusion.
- 250+ kata, 40 menit; rencana 5–8 menit, tulis 27 menit, periksa 5 menit.
- Posisi jelas dan konsisten; ide dikembangkan dengan penjelasan dan contoh.
- Hindari hafalan, kata mutlak, dan ide baru di conclusion.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**

1. *Soal: "Discuss both views and give your opinion." Berapa tugas yang harus dijawab?*
2. *Apa kepanjangan PEEL?*
3. *Kalimat mana pembuka paragraf badan yang baik: "Firstly, excessive screen time can harm children's health." atau "I will write about screens."?*
4. *Apakah boleh memperkenalkan ide baru di conclusion?*
5. *Berapa menit sebaiknya untuk Task 2 dan berapa kata minimum?*

**Kunci:** 1. Tiga (view A, view B, opinimu). 2. Point, Explanation, Example, Link. 3. Yang pertama (topic sentence yang jelas). 4. Tidak. 5. 40 menit dan 250 kata.
