## Tujuan Belajar

Setelah bagian ini, kamu bisa:

1. Memakai **inversion** (pembalikan subjek-auxiliary) setelah ekspresi negatif dan pembatas untuk penekanan, serta pada **conditional formal**.
2. Menyusun **participle clauses** (present, past, perfect participle) untuk menggabungkan ide secara ringkas dan menghindari **dangling participle**.
3. Mengubah klausa menjadi **frasa nomina (nominalization)** agar tulisan lebih padat dan akademik.
4. Memakai ketiganya **secara natural dan hemat** di Writing dan Speaking untuk menunjukkan **Grammatical Range Band 7+**.

> **Ringkasan 30 detik**
> - **Inversion**: *Never **have I seen**...*, *Not only **did he** win...*, *Had I known...* (auxiliary sebelum subjek).
> - **Participle clause**: *Having finished the report, she left.* (subjek **harus sama** dengan klausa utama).
> - **Nominalization**: *The government decided...* → *The government's **decision** to...* (verb/adjective menjadi noun).
> - Pakai **sedikit dan tepat**. Struktur ini menaikkan skor bila akurat, dan menurunkannya bila dipaksakan.

## 1. Mengapa Struktur Lanjutan?

:::compare
Inversion | Auxiliary sebelum subjek: Never have I seen
Participle clause | Subjek harus sama: Having finished, she left
Nominalization | Verb jadi noun: the rapid rise in prices
:::

Penguji menilai **Grammatical Range and Accuracy**. Band 7 mensyaratkan **berbagai struktur kompleks** dengan **akurasi tinggi**. Tiga struktur di bawah ini adalah penanda gaya formal dan akademik yang **melengkapi** conditional, relative clause, dan pasif (Modul 2.1).

| Struktur | Fungsi | Dipakai terutama di |
|---|---|---|
| **Inversion** | Penekanan, formal | Writing Task 2, pidato |
| **Participle clause** | Ringkas, menggabungkan | Writing Task 1/2, Reading |
| **Nominalization** | Padat dan akademik | Writing Task 1/2 akademik |

## 2. Inversion

### 2.1 Pola dasar

Pada inversion, **auxiliary (atau modal) mendahului subjek**, seperti pada kalimat tanya, tetapi kalimatnya tetap pernyataan.

| Normal | Inversion |
|---|---|
| I have **never** seen such a thing. | **Never have I** seen such a thing. |
| She **rarely** complains. | **Rarely does she** complain. |
| He not only won, but also broke the record. | **Not only did he** win, but he also broke the record. |

**Aturan:** jika tidak ada auxiliary, tambahkan **do/does/did**.

### 2.2 Inversion setelah ekspresi negatif dan pembatas

| Ekspresi | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Never** | Never + aux + S + V3/V1 | **Never have** I **felt** so proud. |
| **Rarely / Seldom** | Rarely + aux + S | **Rarely does** this **happen**. |
| **Hardly ... when** | Hardly + had + S + V3 ... when | **Hardly had** we **left** when it rained. |
| **No sooner ... than** | No sooner + had + S + V3 ... than | **No sooner had** she **arrived** than the phone rang. |
| **Not only ... but also** | Not only + aux + S | **Not only did** prices **rise**, but wages also fell. |
| **Under no circumstances** | Under no circumstances + aux + S | **Under no circumstances should** you **share** your password. |
| **Only when / Only after** | Only when + klausa + aux + S | **Only when** it was over **did** she **relax**. |
| **Little** | Little + aux + S | **Little did** he **know** about the plan. |
| **Nowhere / At no time** | Nowhere + aux + S | **Nowhere** is this **more visible** than in cities. |

### 2.3 Inversion pada conditional formal

| Conditional biasa | Inversion (lebih formal) |
|---|---|
| **If I had known**, I would have helped. | **Had I known**, I would have helped. |
| **If it were** not for the funding, the project would fail. | **Were it not for** the funding, the project would fail. |
| **If you should** need help, call us. | **Should you** need help, call us. |

### 2.4 Inversion dengan *so/such*

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **So + adjective + aux + S** | **So rapid was** the change that few were prepared. |
| **Such + be + noun** | **Such was** the demand that stocks sold out. |

:::tips-examiner
Pakai **satu atau dua inversion** per esai, di tempat yang **mendukung argumen**, mis. *Not only does technology improve efficiency, but it also reduces costs.* Banyak inversion dalam satu paragraf terdengar dipaksakan. Dan pastikan **bentuk auxiliary dan urutan kata** benar: satu kesalahan menghapus kesan Band 7.
:::

### 2.5 Variasi contoh inversion

**Level 1: dasar**
1. **Never have I been** to Japan.
2. **Rarely does he** eat out.

**Level 2: sehari-hari (Speaking)**
3. **Not only is** my city beautiful, **but it is also** very safe.
4. **Little did I know** how difficult the course would be.

**Level 3: akademik (Writing)**
5. **Not only does** urbanisation **strain** public services, **but it also** increases pollution.
6. **Hardly had** the policy **been introduced** when opposition began to grow.
7. **Under no circumstances should** governments **ignore** the long-term impact of climate change.
8. **Had** the authorities **acted** earlier, the crisis **could have been avoided**.

## 3. Participle Clauses

### 3.1 Tiga bentuk

| Bentuk | Arti | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Present participle (V-ing)** | Aktif, bersamaan atau sebab | **Walking** home, I met an old friend. |
| **Past participle (V3)** | Pasif | **Built** in 1990, the bridge is still strong. |
| **Perfect participle (having + V3)** | Aktif, lebih dulu | **Having finished** the report, she went home. |
| **Being + V3 / Having been + V3** | Pasif, lebih formal | **Having been warned**, he took care. |

### 3.2 Fungsi

| Fungsi | Klausa panjang | Participle clause |
|---|---|---|
| **Waktu (bersamaan)** | While she was walking home, she met... | **Walking** home, she met... |
| **Waktu (berurutan)** | After he had finished work, he... | **Having finished** work, he... |
| **Sebab** | Because she was tired, she... | **Being** tired, she... / **Tired**, she... |
| **Akibat** | ..., and this resulted in... | ..., **resulting in**... |
| **Kondisi** | If it is used correctly, the tool... | **Used** correctly, the tool... |
| **Menjelaskan (relative)** | The man who stood there... | The man **standing** there... |

### 3.3 Aturan terpenting: subjek harus sama

**Subjek participle clause = subjek klausa utama.** Jika berbeda, terjadi **dangling participle** (kesalahan).

| Salah (dangling) | Benar |
|---|---|
| **Walking** home, the rain started. (hujan berjalan?) | **Walking** home, **I** was caught in the rain. |
| **Having finished** the report, the lights were turned off. | **Having finished** the report, **she** turned off the lights. |
| **Based on** the data, a conclusion was drawn. (lemah) | **Based on** the data, **the researchers** drew a conclusion. |

:::kesalahan-umum
**Dangling participle** adalah kesalahan paling umum pada struktur ini. Tanyakan: *"Siapa yang melakukan tindakan participle?"* Jika bukan subjek kalimat utama, ubah kalimatnya. *Walking home, the rain started* salah karena "hujan" tidak berjalan; *Walking home, I was caught in the rain* benar.
:::

### 3.4 Variasi contoh participle clause

**Level 1: dasar**
1. **Walking** to school, I saw an accident.
2. **Tired** after the trip, he went to bed early.

**Level 2: sehari-hari**
3. **Having lived** in Bandung for ten years, I know the city very well.
4. **Not knowing** what to do, she asked her teacher.

**Level 3: akademik**
5. **Used** effectively, technology can **improve** learning outcomes.
6. **Having examined** the data, the researchers **concluded** that the policy was ineffective.
7. Governments cut spending, **leading to** a rise in unemployment.
8. **Faced with** rising costs, many companies **reduced** their workforce.

## 4. Nominalization

### 4.1 Apa itu?

**Nominalization** = mengubah **verb**, **adjective**, atau **klausa** menjadi **frasa nomina**. Ini menjadikan tulisan **lebih padat, objektif, dan akademik**.

| Klausa (kata kerja) | Frasa nomina |
|---|---|
| The government **decided** to ban plastic. | The government's **decision** to ban plastic... |
| People **are increasing** the use of cars. | The **increase** in car use... |
| Prices **rose** quickly. | The **rapid rise** in prices... |
| It is **important** that we protect forests. | The **importance** of protecting forests... |

### 4.2 Tabel derivasi

| Verb | Noun | Adjective | Noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| **decide** | decision | **important** | importance |
| **develop** | development | **effective** | effectiveness |
| **increase** | increase | **available** | availability |
| **reduce** | reduction | **responsible** | responsibility |
| **pollute** | pollution | **dependent** | dependence |
| **educate** | education | **able** | ability |
| **analyse** | analysis | **different** | difference |
| **fail** | failure | **safe** | safety |

### 4.3 Dari klausa ke kalimat padat

| Gaya sederhana | Gaya akademik (nominalized) |
|---|---|
| Because people **drive** more cars, pollution **increases**. | **The growth in car use** leads to **increased pollution**. |
| Companies **spend** more on research, so products **improve**. | **Higher spending on research** results in **improved products**. |
| When a country **grows quickly**, the environment may **suffer**. | **Rapid economic growth** may have **a damaging effect** on the environment. |

### 4.4 Kata kerja pendamping nominalization

| Kata kerja | Contoh |
|---|---|
| **lead to / result in** | **Population growth** leads to **housing shortages**. |
| **cause / contribute to** | **Deforestation** contributes to **climate change**. |
| **account for** | **Rising costs** account for **the decline** in sales. |
| **be associated with** | **Obesity** is associated with **inactivity**. |
| **depend on** | **Success** depends on **the availability** of resources. |

:::tips-examiner
Nominalization bagus untuk **Task 2 dan Task 1 Academic**, tetapi tulisan yang **terlalu banyak noun** terasa berat dan sulit dibaca. Seimbangkan dengan kata kerja yang kuat (*Rapid urbanisation **strains** public services*). Satu kalimat nominal yang jelas lebih baik daripada tiga kalimat yang padat dan membingungkan.
:::

## 5. Menggabungkan Ketiganya

| Kalimat sederhana | Versi lanjutan |
|---|---|
| Cars cause pollution. Governments should act. They have not. | **Not only do cars cause serious pollution, but governments have also failed to act.** (inversion) |
| Technology has changed work. People use it a lot. | **Having transformed the workplace, technology is now used by a growing number of people.** (participle) |
| People decided to move to cities. This created a shortage of housing. | **The decision to move to cities has created a shortage of housing.** (nominalization) |
| Prices rise. People spend less. | **A rise in prices leads to reduced spending.** (nominalization) |

## 6. Contoh Jawaban Model: Tiga Level Band

**Soal:** *Some people believe that cities are growing too fast. Write one body paragraph.*

- **Band 5:** Cities grow fast. Many people come. It is a problem. Pollution is increase and houses are expensive.
- **Band 6:** Cities are growing very quickly because many people move there for work. This creates problems such as pollution and expensive housing. The government should do something about it.
- **Band 7:** Not only does rapid urbanisation place considerable pressure on public services, but it also drives up housing costs. Attracted by better job prospects, millions of people migrate to cities each year, resulting in overcrowding and increased pollution. Had governments invested earlier in affordable housing, many of these problems might have been avoided.

**Mengapa naik band?** (1) **Inversion** (*Not only does..., but...; Had governments invested*), (2) **participle** (*Attracted by..., resulting in...*), (3) **nominalization** (*rapid urbanisation, increased pollution*), (4) tetap akurat dan terbaca natural.

## 7. Kesalahan Umum Pelajar Indonesia

| # | Kesalahan | Perbaikan | Penjelasan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Never I have seen | **Never have I** seen | Auxiliary sebelum subjek |
| 2 | Not only he won | **Not only did he** win | Tambah *did* |
| 3 | Rarely she complains | **Rarely does she** complain | Inversion |
| 4 | Hardly had we left **than** it rained | Hardly had we left **when** it rained | *Hardly ... when* |
| 5 | **No sooner** ... **when** | No sooner ... **than** | Pasangan tetap |
| 6 | **Walking** home, the rain started | Walking home, **I** was caught in the rain | Dangling participle |
| 7 | **Having finished** the lights were off | Having finished, **she** turned off the lights | Subjek sama |
| 8 | **Being tired**, the exam was difficult | **Being tired**, **I** found the exam difficult | Subjek sama |
| 9 | **Had I knew** | **Had I known** | V3 setelah *had* |
| 10 | Inversion di **setiap** kalimat | Satu atau dua per esai | Dipaksakan |
| 11 | **The decide** of the government | The **decision** of the government | Kelas kata |
| 12 | Terlalu banyak **noun** berantai | Sisipkan kata kerja | Terlalu padat |

## 8. Strategi IELTS: Cara Berlatih dan Memakai

### 8.1 Latihan sehari-hari

| Latihan | Cara |
|---|---|
| **Ubah kalimat** | Ambil 5 kalimat sederhana; ubah menjadi inversion, participle, dan nominal |
| **Cari dangling** | Periksa tulisanmu: siapa subjek tiap participle? |
| **Baca artikel akademik** | Tandai participle clauses dan frasa nomina |
| **Tulis paragraf** | Pakai satu struktur lanjutan per kalimat kunci, bukan semuanya |
| **Bandingkan** | Tulis versi sederhana dan versi lanjutan; pilih yang jelas |

### 8.2 Saat tes

- **Writing Task 2:** pakai satu inversion (*Not only..., but also*) dan dua participle atau nominalization.
- **Writing Task 1 Academic:** participle untuk menggabungkan data (*..., reaching a peak of...*) dan nominal (*a sharp rise in*).
- **Speaking:** inversion ringan (*Not only..., but...*) sesekali; jangan dipaksakan.
- **Cek akhir:** subjek participle sama? auxiliary dan urutan kata inversion benar?

## 9. Ringkasan dan Cek Diri

**Ingat:**
- **Inversion:** ekspresi negatif/pembatas + auxiliary + subjek; conditional formal (*Had I known, Were it not for, Should you*).
- **Participle clause:** subjek **harus sama**; present, past, dan perfect participle.
- **Nominalization:** verb/adjective → noun untuk padat dan akademik; jaga keseimbangan.
- Pakai sedikit dan akurat.

**Cek diri (tanpa melihat materi):**
1. *Ubah ke inversion: "I have never seen such a thing."*
2. *Mengapa "Walking home, the rain started" salah?*
3. *Pasangan yang benar: "No sooner ... (when/than)".*
4. *Nominalkan: "Prices rose quickly."*
5. *Ubah ke bentuk formal: "If I had known, I would have helped."*

**Kunci:** 1. Never have I seen such a thing. 2. Subjeknya berbeda (dangling participle). 3. than. 4. The rapid rise in prices. 5. Had I known, I would have helped.
