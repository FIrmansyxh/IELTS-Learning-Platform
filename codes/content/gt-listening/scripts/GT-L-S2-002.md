[A] Good evening, and thanks for tuning in to Westbridge Community Radio. I'm Martin Hale, and tonight I've got all the information you need about this year's Summer Food and Craft Festival, which takes place this coming Saturday.
[A] The first and most important thing to know is that the festival has a new home this year. For the past ten years it has been held in the Market Square, but because of the road works on the high street, the organisers have moved it to Victoria Park. So please don't go into the town centre on Saturday expecting to find the stalls there.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] The gates will open at nine thirty in the morning. In the programme it says nine o'clock, but that was a printing mistake, so do make a note of it. Things will finish at around six in the evening.
[A] This year is bigger than ever. Last year there were about fifty stalls, and this time there will be more than eighty, selling everything from local cheese and fresh bread to hand-made jewellery and pottery.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] Now, because the park is quite large, let me tell you where the main attractions are. When you come in through the main gate, the first thing you'll see is the information tent, where you can pick up a map. The children's craft tent won't be near the entrance as it was last year. Instead, you'll find it in the rose garden, which is a quieter area, away from the traffic.
[A] The live music stage has been placed beside the lake, so you can listen to the bands while you sit on the grass by the water. And if anyone feels unwell or has a minor accident, the first aid point is next to the car park, so that an ambulance can reach it easily if necessary.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] The organisers are trying to make this a greener festival. None of the food and drink stalls will be handing out disposable cups this year. So, if you want a drink, please remember to bring a reusable cup with you. If you forget, you can buy one at the information tent for a pound.
[A] As for getting there, I'm afraid parking will be very limited. The small car park at the park is reserved mainly for stallholders and disabled visitors. Taxis won't be able to drop people at the gate either, because Park Lane will be closed to traffic all day. So the organisers are strongly recommending two options. The first is the free shuttle bus, which will run every fifteen minutes from the railway station. The second is to cycle, and there will be plenty of secure bike racks just inside the main gate.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] What about the cost? Well, children under sixteen get in free, and adults are asked to pay three pounds at the gate. All the money from ticket sales will go to the Westbridge Hospice, so it's for a very good cause.
[A] Finally, the organisers are still looking for volunteers to help with setting up on Friday evening and with collecting litter during the day. If you can spare a few hours, please contact the festival coordinator, Mrs Thornbury. That's T-H-O-R-N-B-U-R-Y. She'd be delighted to hear from you, and volunteers get a free lunch.
[A] So, that's the Summer Food and Craft Festival this Saturday in Victoria Park. Let's hope the sun shines. Now, here's some music to get you in the mood.
