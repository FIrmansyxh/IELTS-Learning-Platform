[A] Thanks for coming in, Marcus, Jenny. I wanted to catch up with you both about the food safety training before things get busy next month.
[B] Sure, Linda. We've both started the online part already.
[A] Great. Before we get into the details, I know some people in the kitchen think we're doing this because of the complaint we got last month about the cold buffet.
[C] Yeah, that's what everyone's been saying.
[A] Well, it's not true. That complaint was dealt with separately. The real reason is that the city council has introduced a new regulation. From this year, every member of staff who handles food has to hold a food safety certificate, not just the supervisors.
[B] Oh, I see. So it's a legal requirement now.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] Exactly. So, how are you finding the online course?
[C] It's okay. My friend did it at another hotel a couple of years ago, and she said there were eight modules, but there are only six now.
[A] That's right. They combined some of the shorter ones, so it's six modules in total. And you need to finish all of them by the end of February. I can't give any extensions this time, because the inspection is in early March.
[B] The end of February. Okay, that's plenty of time.
[A] Have you found any of the topics difficult so far?
[B] For me, the section on allergens was the hardest. There are fourteen of them, and you have to know exactly which dishes on our menu contain each one.
[C] I agree about allergens. And I struggled with temperature control, too. All those different temperatures for storing, cooking, and reheating food are hard to remember.
[B] I found the temperature part okay, actually. But neither of us had any trouble with the cleaning module.
[C] No, that was easy. And pest control was mostly common sense.
[pause 2.0s]
[A] Good to know. I'll ask the trainer to spend extra time on those two areas. Now, after the online modules, there's a practical session. It's a half-day session with a trainer from the college.
[C] Will that be in the main kitchen?

[B] Good idea. There's lots of space down there.
[C] Do we need to bring anything with us on the day?
[A] Just your usual uniform and non-slip shoes. The college will provide aprons, hair nets, and all the thermometers and equipment. Oh, and please bring a notebook, because the trainer will give you a lot of practical tips that aren't in the online material.

[A] There'll be a short break halfway through, and the kitchen team will leave some sandwiches for you, so you won't go hungry.
