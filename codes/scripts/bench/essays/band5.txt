Nowadays many people think the government should spend more money on public transport and not on building new roads. I agree with this opinion because there are some reason.

First, public transport is good for the environment. When people use bus or train, there are less car in the street and the air is more clean. In my city, the air is very bad because every person have a car. If the government build more roads, more people will buy car and the problem will become worse. Public transport can carry many people in one time so it is more efficient.

Second, public transport is cheaper for the people. Petrol is expensive and parking cost a lot of money in the center of the city. A student or a worker who does not earn much money can not pay for this. If there is a good train, they can go to school or office with small money. Also, old people and people who is poor can travel easy.

However, some people say that roads is important too, because the truck need them to bring food and goods. This is true, but the government can repair the old roads and does not need to build many new one.

In conclusion, I think public transport is more better than new roads because it help the environment and it is cheap for everybody. The government should put more money for bus and train in the future.
